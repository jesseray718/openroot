# Why OpenRoot Exists
Knowledge is the only resource that multiplies when shared. Copying a design costs nothing — so the essentials of life (shelter, energy, water, food, knowledge, computation) can be driven toward zero cost for everyone, permanently. We don't redistribute wealth; we de-commodify survival.

**Permaculture is the operating system:** catch & store (git, caches, local AI on rescued hardware) · produce no waste (e-waste is refined ore) · small & slow (a 1.5B model on a $150 phone) · use edges (offline knowledge, mesh, libraries) · integrate (protocols over platforms) · every element stacks functions.

**The floor rises even when the ceiling rises.** Frontier AI trains once for billions; open weights then run at the edge forever, free. Our job is the edge.

**The acropolis is shared high ground:** The Hearth in the physical world, this repository in the digital one. Humanity unites around a build anyone can start Saturday morning — the 1v flat-pack shelter is the on-ramp; Cloud Nine (concept: square-cube buoyancy at kilometer scale — unbuilt, honest moonshot) is the horizon marker. Everything between them is labeled exactly what it is: concept, in-progress, tested, or validated.

Maximum good, most people, least effort. Fork it.
