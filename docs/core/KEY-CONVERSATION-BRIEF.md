# OpenRoot — Key Conversation Brief

**One Human Family | July 2026**

## The Core Inversion

Most systems extract value at every layer (housing, energy, communication, food, care, governance).  
OpenRoot **eliminates** those extraction layers and replaces them with systems that reward contribution and care.

We are not asking people to sacrifice. We are offering a cascading series of liberations where each system built removes a cost and generates ongoing value for the people who build it.

## How Value Flows (Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Model)

Anyone who builds receives **lifetime affiliate income** from what they create — whether they joined yesterday or years ago.

- Build a workshop, dome, thermal system, or mesh node → earn ongoing percentage from its output
- Provide care work → compensated on the ledger at premium rates
- Generate knowledge or food → minted as ACRE with transparent provenance

This replaces rent-seeking, debt, and welfare with direct, contribution-based economics. Trust and reputation become revenue instead of a cost.

## The Full Stack

OpenRoot is not a single project. It is an integrated stack:

- **Economic Layer** — Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Coins (replaces taxation and extraction)
- **Organizational Layer** — Teams of 50 with built-in mutual aid and care roles
- **Physical Layer** — Geodesic domes + ferrocement stress-skin shelters + H-003 thermal systems
- **Preparedness Layer** — 501c3 for land, long-term food/medicinal storage, and hardened community infrastructure
- **Governance Layer** — Kingdom Engine + transparent on-chain reputation (replaces failed government functions)

## Why This Matters Now

Current systems are extracting roughly $37–45+ trillion per year globally through overlapping layers of housing, energy, finance, healthcare, and governance. Most of this value never returns to the communities that generate it.

OpenRoot offers a practical, buildable path to redirect a meaningful portion of that value back into the places where people actually live — while creating stronger, more resilient communities in the process.

We are not waiting for permission. We are building the alternative in public, starting from one node in Sikeston, Missouri.
