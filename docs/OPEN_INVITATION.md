# Open Invitation — Graded Contribution Pipeline

OpenRoot is open to builders. Contributions are graded, not judged.

## The ladder
1. **Anyone** forks, builds, replicates. Evidence earns rungs.
2. **Grading**: measured data > bench test > reasoned hypothesis. No hype, no >100% thermo.
3. **Credit is architectural**: contributors (e.g., Reh1t, PR #53) are named in-tree, not just thanked.
4. **Non-recompute**: verified solutions are SHA-256 paired with their mistakes — your win compounds for everyone after you.

## Start here
See GOOD_FIRST_ISSUES.md and CONTRIBUTING.md. Build something. Report what happened.
The goal of the ladder: ideas become things strangers can pick up and build.
