# Contribution Workflow

## Purpose

Convert OpenRoot work into small, evidence-labeled contributions without
overloading maintainers or creating unmaintainable commitments.

## Workflow

1. Inventory a candidate contribution.
2. Record source, license, evidence level, safety implications, and scope.
3. Identify one appropriate target project or mark it "keep internal."
4. Ask a maintainer or community where the fit is uncertain.
5. Draft the smallest useful issue or pull request.
6. Submit only after human review and explicit approval.
7. Record feedback, accepted changes, declined proposals, and maintenance work.

## Evidence labels

- `OBSERVED`: Directly measured; include date, method, instrument, and raw data.
- `SOURCED`: Supported by a credible cited source.
- `SIMULATED`: Model output with stated assumptions and model version.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: Plausible but untested.
- `VISION`: A value, goal, or future-state proposal.

## External contribution rule

Do not send project-specific branding, philosophy, or unvalidated systems claims
to another project unless its maintainers request or approve that context.
