THE COPPICING ADVANTAGE
Traditional Firewood (Carbon-Neutral at Best)
Tree planted → 20 years growth → Tree killed → Wood dried → Burned → CO₂ released
Carbon Balance: Neutral (new tree must regrow to offset emissions)
Black Locust Coppicing (Carbon Negative)
Tree planted → Year 1: harvest shoots → Roots grow MORE → Year 2: bigger shoot yield
Year 3-100: Annual harvest without killing tree → Root system expands continuously
Carbon Balance: NEGATIVE — Roots keep sequestering while shoots release stored energy
Math:

1 hectare black locust plantation
Annual harvest: 15-20 dry tons of wood
Burning releases: \~25 MWh thermal
Meanwhile: Root system adds 30 tons C/hectare to soil
Net: -15 tons C/hectare/year (carbon sink)
