## Phase 2 — Regional Rollout (Months 7-24)

**Objective:** 25,641 superhubs + 28,517 urban relays covering 50% inhabited land.

**Rural (23,077 superhubs)**
- Crowd-funded or DAO-funded deployments in 195 countries.
- Each superhub = 12-dish multi-role node ($600) or ultra-optimized \~$58 version.
- Spacing: \~15 km (LoRa). Prioritize water towers, hills, existing tall structures.
- Target: 50% of rural land area.

**Urban (2,564 superhubs + 28,517 relays)**
- Major metro areas first (density >1,000 people/km²).
- Superhubs at 3-4.5 km spacing.
- Relays = 3-6 dish upgrades on volunteer rooftops ($111-222 each).
- Household dish kits sold/distributed at cost ($37).

**ACRE Alignment**
- Backbone builders (superhubs) receive 10× base mint rate (T2 verified).
- Relays receive 5× base.
- All uptime + packets relayed logged for proportional mint.
- Kingdom Engine two-approval process documented and tested in this phase.

**Success metric:** 50% inhabited land covered. First 10,000 household leaves online. ACRE minting live and audited.

## Honest Limitations (from OPTIMIZED-MINIMUM-MESH-COST.md)

- LoRa = 250 kbps rural (text/sensor level). Video needs urban WiFi.
- Organic growth is slower than top-down. Relies on human will.
- Regulatory variance by country — political work required in parallel.
- Supply chain for 300K+ dishes at scale.
- Volunteer maintenance burden — neighbor redundancy + regional repair hubs mitigate.

All solvable. The math is settled. The technology exists. The variable left is execution.
