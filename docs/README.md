## Key Principles

- **Agape**: Unconditional mutual care as both ethic and optimization strategy.
- **Teams of 50**: Primary organizational unit with built-in mutual aid and support roles.
- **Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Coins**: Builders and caregivers earn lifetime rewards from what they create.
- **Cascading Liberation**: Each system built eliminates a cost that funds the next layer of independence.
- **One Human Family**: We cut what harms and nourish what serves the whole.
