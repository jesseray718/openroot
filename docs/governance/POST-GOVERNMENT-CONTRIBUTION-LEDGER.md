# Post-Government Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Coin System

**UNE:** DV.GOV.POST01
**Date:** 2026-07-08

Current governments have failed. We stop feeding them. We replace extraction with direct contribution, transparent ledgers, and lifetime rewards for anyone who builds.

## Core Principle

We act as One Human Family — one human species. When we organize this way, necessity becomes the mother of invention for the greater good of all. Contributions become more significant because they serve the whole rather than private extraction. We cut away what does not produce fruit and nourish what does.

## The System: Transparent Contribution Ledger + Coins

A public, immutable ledger tracks real contribution. When work is completed, coins are minted. Value flows visibly. Everyone can see where resources come from and where they go.

**Coins / Tokens (modular or unified)**
- Work Coin — for any productive labor
- Knowledge / Scientific Coin — for research, documentation, plant breeding, etc.
- Health & Nutrition Coin — for meals, detox protocols, herbal work
- Infrastructure Coin — for workshops, tool libraries, seed banks, mesh nodes, thermal systems
- Affiliate Coin (separate or integrated) — automatically tracks and distributes ongoing rewards from what you build

## Lifetime Affiliate Model (Anyone Who Builds Gets Paid)

This is the key upgrade:

If you build something — whether you are the first person or the most recent person to join — you receive lifetime affiliate income from what you personally create.

Examples:
- You build 10 community workshops → You receive ongoing affiliate percentage from everything produced by those 10 workshops for the rest of your life.
- You install the first local solar array or help create a thermal cascade system → You receive affiliate from the energy/value that system produces.
- You start a seed bank or medicinal herb project → You receive affiliate from seeds, plants, and knowledge distributed from it.
- You help build a community kitchen or health protocol center → You receive affiliate from its ongoing use.

**How it works technically:**
- Every major project or piece of infrastructure is registered on the ledger.
- An affiliate percentage (starting suggestion: 2%) flows automatically to everyone who contributed to building it.
- The ledger tracks interconnected value so contributions can be fairly attributed even in complex, multi-person projects.
- This replaces pensions, welfare, and most redistribution with direct, contribution-based lifetime income.

The more valuable and long-lasting what you build, the more you are rewarded over time. Late joiners are not disadvantaged — they simply start earning from what they build from that point forward.

## Teams of 50 + Agape Mutual Aid

Every team of 50 is organized to produce value and care for its members. When any team or community is in need, other teams send resources, knowledge, and people. No one is left to struggle alone. This living mutual aid replaces the failed welfare and bureaucratic state.

## Burning Non-Productive Branches

We stop maintaining systems that extract without producing real value for the people:
- Victimless crime laws and related prisons → Removed
- Corporate welfare and monopoly protection → Removed
- Endless extraction through taxation and inflation → Replaced by direct contribution

What remains focuses on protecting people from harm and enabling productive, caring contribution.

## Feedback Loops & Greater Good

As we act as One Human Family, necessity shifts. Invention and contribution become oriented toward the well-being of all rather than private profit. The ledger makes second and third-order effects visible so the system can continuously improve. What produces good fruit for the whole is nourished. What does not is cut away.
