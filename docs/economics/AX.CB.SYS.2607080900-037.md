# AX-037 — The Credit Float

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

When a 0% APR instrument is used for a purchase, capital that would have been spent is retained in an interest-bearing account for the duration of the grace period. The float yield is the interest earned on money that has been "spent" but not yet left your possession. This is arbitrage on timing.

## Derivation

0% APR credit card grants a grace period of 21-25 days after statement close before payment is due. If $500 is charged on day 1 and the statement closes on day 30, the $500 can sit in a 4% APY savings account for 21-55 days before the payment is due. Float yield = principal × APY × (days/365).

## Application

When 0% APR instruments become available (typically at 700+ score), route large purchases through them. Retain cash in interest-bearing accounts until payment is due. The float yield is small per transaction but compounds across volume.
