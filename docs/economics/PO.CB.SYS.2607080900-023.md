# PO-023 — The Autonomous Credit Machine

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

When three conditions are met simultaneously — (1) total credit limits exceed monthly spending by 10×, (2) all recurring expenses are autopaid through credit instruments, and (3) all card balances are autopaid in full from checking — the credit-building system becomes self-executing. No further conscious action is required. Score increases are generated passively by the mechanical operation of the system.

## Derivation

Condition 1 ensures utilization is naturally below 10% without management. Condition 2 ensures continuous payment history generation. Condition 3 ensures zero late payments and zero interest. Together, the system optimizes all controllable FICO factors automatically. The only remaining variable is time (age), which accrues passively by AX-CB-006.

## Application

Target: $10,000+ total credit limits, all bills on autopay through cards, all cards on autopay from checking. Once achieved, the system runs itself. Conscious credit management becomes unnecessary. Score increases are mechanical and inevitable.
