# PO-024 — The Convergence Point

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

At the intersection of personal score ≥720 and business PAYDEX ≥80, a borrower can access: unsecured personal cards at prime rates, unsecured business cards, business auto loans under EIN, equipment financing, SBA microloans, and DSCR/commercial property loans. This convergence point is reachable within 12-18 months from a standing start of 660 personal / zero business using the cascading framework.

## Derivation

720 personal crosses the "good credit" tier gate (AX-CB-010), unlocking prime-rate unsecured products. PAYDEX 80 represents perfect business payment history, unlocking business lending products. Together they enable EIN-based borrowing with or without personal guarantee, and DSCR qualification based on property cash flow rather than personal income.

## Application

Track both metrics in parallel. Personal score via Credit Karma/Kikoff dashboard. Business PAYDEX via Nav/CreditSignal. Both must reach threshold simultaneously for convergence to activate. Timeline: 12-18 months from standing start.
