# PO-020 — The Double-Yield Dollar

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

If a recurring payment (e.g., $35 Kikoff subscription) is charged to a credit-building card (e.g., Varo Believe) that reports to all three bureaus, the same dollar generates two independent credit events: the Kikoff-reported payment AND the Believe-reported transaction. No additional spending is required. One dollar, two tradeline yields.

## Derivation

Kikoff reports the $35 monthly payment as an on-time payment to Equifax, Experian, TransUnion. Varo Believe reports the $35 charge + repayment as a separate on-time payment to the same three bureaus. The $35 exits Varo checking once, but two tradelines register activity. The credit yield per dollar is doubled without any increase in spending.

## Application

Update Kikoff payment method to Varo Believe card. Every month, one $35 payment generates two bureau-reported credit events. This is the highest-yield configuration available with existing instruments.
