# AX-033 — The Limit Expansion Cascade

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Each new credit line increases total available credit. If spending remains constant while total limit rises, utilization drops proportionally across all accounts. Lower aggregate utilization raises the score, which unlocks higher-limit products, which further lowers utilization. This is a self-reinforcing upward spiral.

## Derivation

Aggregate utilization = total balances / total limits. Increasing denominator (limits) while holding numerator (balances) constant decreases the ratio. Lower utilization → higher score → qualification for higher-limit products → larger denominator → lower utilization → higher score. The spiral is positive and self-reinforcing.

## Application

Accept all credit limit increases offered. Do not reduce credit limits voluntarily. Each new account opened expands total available credit, compressing utilization across all existing accounts.
