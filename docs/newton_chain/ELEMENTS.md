# Newton Chain Elements

A conceptual, Euclid-style derivation framework for organizing definitions, assumptions, evidence, and verification receipts. It is not a claim of universal physical derivation.

## Canonical order

1. Definitions
2. Axioms
3. Postulates
4. Propositions
5. Proofs
6. Corollaries
7. Scholia

## Rule

No proposition is accepted as proven unless its proof names its dependencies
and its verification context.

No analogy is treated as proof.

No physical interpretation is treated as physical proof without stated,
measurable conditions and a corresponding test or observation.
