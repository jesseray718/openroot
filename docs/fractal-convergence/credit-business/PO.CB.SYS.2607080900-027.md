# PO-027 — The Utilization Floor Effect

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

When total available credit across all accounts exceeds $10,000, normal monthly spending of $400-600 naturally falls below 6% utilization without any conscious management. The system self-regulates utilization through scale alone. Active utilization management becomes unnecessary above this threshold.

## Derivation

$500 monthly spending ÷ $10,000 total limits = 5% utilization. This is below the 10% optimal threshold (AX-CB-002) automatically. No payment timing strategy, no pre-statement paydown, no split payments needed. Scale solves the utilization problem permanently.

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-002 (Utilization Threshold)
- AX-CB-009 (Limit Expansion Cascade)

## Application

Prioritize reaching $10,000 total credit limits. Until then, manage utilization manually. After $10K threshold, utilization management ceases to be a relevant operational concern for typical spending levels.
