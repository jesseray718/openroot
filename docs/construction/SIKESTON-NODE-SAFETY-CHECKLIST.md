## Pre-Work Safety (Before Starting)

- [ ] Reviewed full Risk Assessment Matrix and understand high-risk items
- [ ] Confirmed second person/spotter will be present for work at height or electrical work
- [ ] Inspected all tools (jigsaw, drill, etc.) for damage or defects
- [ ] PPE ready: safety glasses, work gloves, closed-toe shoes, high-visibility clothing
- [ ] Checked weather forecast — no high wind, rain, or storms planned for mounting
- [ ] Confirmed mounting location is structurally sound and free of overhead hazards (power lines, etc.)
- [ ] Prepared basic first-aid kit and emergency contacts
- [ ] Emergency descent plan reviewed (if working at height)

## During Construction (Workshop / Ground Level)

- [ ] Wearing safety glasses when cutting plywood or chicken wire
- [ ] Wearing gloves when handling chicken wire or sharp edges
- [ ] Work area kept clean and free of trip hazards
- [ ] Materials and tools secured so nothing can fall or shift unexpectedly
- [ ] Never working on live electrical circuits (solar panel disconnected when wiring)
- [ ] Using outdoor-rated cables and proper weatherproof enclosures
- [ ] Power system (solar + battery) tested on the ground before mounting

## During Mounting & Installation (On Site)

- [ ] Stable, rated ladder or lift being used
- [ ] Spotter present on the ground at all times when working above 6 ft
- [ ] All tools and the dish secured with tethers or ropes
- [ ] Mounting hardware (U-bolts, brackets, fasteners) rated for structural outdoor use
- [ ] All fasteners double-checked and torqued after initial mounting
- [ ] Cable entry points protected from water and animals
- [ ] Final visual safety inspection performed from the ground before leaving site
- [ ] Node is stable and cannot swing or fall if a mounting point shifts

## Post-Installation Safety

- [ ] All mounting hardware re-inspected after 24–48 hours
- [ ] Power system verified to run autonomously for minimum 7 days
- [ ] All cables secured and protected from weather, wind, and potential damage
- [ ] Full documentation completed (photos, GPS, signal reports, signed checklists)
- [ ] Emergency shutdown procedure created and stored with node records
- [ ] Periodic re-inspection schedule set (monthly for first 3 months)
