## Core Housing Technology

**Geodesic Domes with Upward/Outward Bevel Architecture**
- Structure: Geodesic dome using ferrocement + graphite + glass-fiber reinforced aerated concrete (AE-GFRC).
- Architectural feature: Upward/outward bevel (biddle-style) on lower sections to improve passive solar gain, water shedding, and structural performance.
- Material advantage: AE-GFRC can be pumped vertically and autonomously during construction, dramatically reducing labor and enabling rapid, high-quality builds.
- Benefits: Excellent strength-to-weight ratio, superior thermal mass, long lifespan (100+ years), low maintenance, and compatibility with integrated thermal labyrinths.

These domes are not isolated houses. They are designed to function as interconnected nodes in the larger permaculture and fractal mesh system.

**Crew Composition (example)**

- Logistics & material handling
- Health & safety / vitals monitoring
- Food preparation (chefs)
- Documentation & knowledge capture
- Community outreach & coordination
- Senior mentors / quality control (experienced builders)
- Caregivers for any vulnerable team members or future residents

**Operating Principles**
- Every crew takes care of its own members.
- Crews support other crews when needed.
- All work is logged on the contribution ledger.
- Builders receive lifetime affiliate rewards from the domes and systems they create (via the Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Coin system).

## Interconnected Housing Model

Domes are built to work together:
- Shared or linked H-003 thermal cascades for heating, cooling, and mechanical work.
- Fractal mesh nodes integrated into every structure for communication and coordination.
- Local food production, water systems, and energy sharing between neighboring domes.
- Mutual aid networks so that when one household or individual needs support, nearby domes/crews can respond quickly.

This creates resilient clusters rather than isolated houses.

## Passive Solar Optimization in Permaculture

Every dome and surrounding permaculture system is designed with maximum passive solar performance:
- Optimal orientation and bevel angles for winter solar gain and summer shading.
- Thermal mass integration (AE-GFRC + underground labyrinths).
- Natural ventilation and daylighting strategies.
- Food forests, water systems, and animal integration positioned to work with solar patterns.
- Goal: Dramatically reduce or eliminate the need for active heating, cooling, and lighting in most climates.
