## The Landauer Limit

Landauer’s principle states that any irreversible logical operation has a minimum thermodynamic cost:

\[ kT \ln 2 \]

per bit erased, where \( k \) is Boltzmann’s constant and \( T \) is temperature.
This is a real physical bound on the energy cost of irreversible computation and of any process that discards information.

Most existing technological and civilizational systems operate far above this bound and continuously generate entropy that must be paid for in energy, materials, and disorder.
