# Anti-Capture & Anti-Exploitation Rules

These rules are hard-coded and require super-majority of *unique small members* (not capital weight) to change.

1. **Capital vs Governance Separation**
   - Voting / decision power is based on unique member identity + time + activity, not dollars invested.
   - Large capital does not buy larger governance weight.

2. **Whale Caps**
   - No single member may hold more than 4.9% of total pool capital.
   - Excess is automatically redistributed or rejected.

3. **Quadratic / Diminishing Influence**
   - Decision coefficient uses diminishing returns on capital contribution.
   - Formula deliberately penalizes concentration.

4. **Cooling & Vesting**
   - Large withdrawals or influence changes require multi-cycle cooling periods.
   - Sudden capital dumps cannot instantly seize control.

5. **Unique-Member Super-Majority**
   - Rule changes require approval from a majority of distinct small members, not majority of capital.

6. **Local-First + Offline Capable**
   - Core ledger and decision engine run fully offline.
   - No single remote server that can be seized or compelled.

7. **Transparent Append-Only Ledger**
   - Every contribution, dividend, hand-up, and rule attempt is permanently recorded.
   - Hash-chained for integrity.

8. **No Preferred Class**
   - No founder shares, no special admin keys, no corporate entity above the ledger.

9. **Hand-up is Non-Negotiable**
   - Fixed percentage of surplus is automatically directed to highest need-coefficient members / communities.
   - Cannot be voted away by capital interests.

These measures make whale takeover, regulatory capture of a single point, and brute-force concentration economically and structurally expensive.
