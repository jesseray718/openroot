# Business Leveling Cooperative — Handbook

## Purpose
Pool small capital from many operators to raise business credit, form LLCs, obtain net-30 accounts, equipment, and operating runway — while preventing any single actor from capturing the system.

## Entry
cd $HOME/openroot/business-leveling
bash handbook/GET-TERMINAL-AND-RUN.sh

## Core Commands
bash zero-dep-app/pool.sh status
bash zero-dep-app/pool.sh invest 100.00 "LLC formation seed"
bash zero-dep-app/pool.sh ledger
bash zero-dep-app/pool.sh dividend
bash zero-dep-app/pool.sh handup

## Critical Rules
- You invest capital only. Never upload EINs, SSNs, or full business credit files into the pool.
- Whale cap: no member > 4.9% of total capital.
- Governance power ≠ capital size.
- Hand-up percentage is automatic and fixed.
- Everything is local-first and auditable.
