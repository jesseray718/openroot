# Hi, I'm Jesse 👋

Imagine a neighborhood where:

- **Food grows everywhere** — in parks, empty lots, front yards — anyone can eat
- **Energy comes from the sun**, stored in the ground, powers homes through winter
- **The internet works even if Comcast goes down** — neighbors routers talk to each other
- **Nobody owns the system** — it belongs to everyone
- **The people who build it benefit from it**

That's what I'm working toward. It's called **OpenRoot**.

---

### Why does this matter?

Almost everything we need — food, energy, internet, housing — is controlled by big companies. We pay them every month just to exist. What if we didn't need them? What if a neighborhood could grow its own food, generate its own power, run its own internet, and build its own homes using materials other people threw away?

---

### What's being built?

🌱 **Permaculture** — Designing landscapes that work like forests. Nobody fertilizes a forest. It just works.

☀️ **Solar Thermal Energy** — Concrete absorbs heat from the sun, stores it underground, releases it through winter. No gas bill, no electric bill.

📡 **Mesh Internet** — A web of small routers talks to each other. If one breaks, signal finds another path. Built from recycled electronics.

🏠 **Indestructible Housing** — Ferrocement dome panels that bolt together like LEGOs. Resist hurricanes, earthquakes, fire.

🤝 **Organization Without Bosses** — Open-source like Wikipedia. Anyone contributes, no one controls it.

---

### Where does money come from?

1. **Electronics recycling** — collecting old tech, destroying data, repurposing hardware
2. **Internet service** — mesh network provides $15/mo instead of $60/mo Comcast
3. **Construction** — concrete contracting under the LLC
4. **Government grants** — rural broadband, renewable energy, community development
5. **Donations** — people who believe in it support it

Money goes back in — more land, tools, nodes. It compounds.

---

### What does OpenRoot mean?

**Root** — going back to the fundamentals. Food, shelter, energy, community, knowledge. **Open** — open to everyone.

---

I'm one guy with a phone, building something that could change how communities work. We pay companies for things we could produce ourselves. We throw away materials we could build with. We depend on systems we don't control.

**OpenRoot is about taking that control back — together, from the ground up.**
