# Hi, I'm Jesse 👋

## And this is what I'm building.

### The Simple Version

Imagine a neighborhood where food grows everywhere, energy comes from the sun, the internet works without Comcast, nobody owns the system, and the people who build it benefit from it. That's OpenRoot.

### Why does this matter?

Right now almost everything we need — food, energy, internet, housing — is controlled by big companies. We pay them every month to exist. What if we didn't need them? What if a neighborhood could grow its own food, generate its own power, run its own internet, and build its own homes using materials other people threw away?

### What's being built?

🌱 **Permaculture Systems** — Designing landscapes that work like natural ecosystems. Forests don't need fertilizer or watering — they just work. Neighborhoods could too.

☀️ **Solar Thermal Energy** — Concrete absorbs heat from the sun, stores it underground, releases it through winter. No gas bill, no electric bill, no firewood.

📡 **Mesh Internet** — Instead of one wire from Comcast, a web of small routers talks to each other. If one breaks, signal finds another path. Built from recycled electronics.

🏠 **Indestructible Housing** — Ferrocement dome panels that bolt together like LEGOs. Domes resist hurricanes, earthquakes, and fire.

🤝 **Organization Without Bosses** — Open-source, like Wikipedia. Anyone contributes, anyone uses, no one person controls it.

### Where does money come from?

1. Electronics recycling — collecting old tech, destroying data securely, repurposing hardware
2. Internet service — mesh network provides $15/mo internet instead of $60/mo
3. Construction — concrete contracting under the LLC
4. Government grants — rural broadband, renewable energy, community development
5. Open-source donations — people who believe in the project support it

Money goes back into the system — more land, more tools, more nodes. It compounds.

### What does OpenRoot mean?

**Root** — going back to the fundamentals. What do humans actually need? Food, shelter, energy, community, knowledge. **Open** — open to everyone. Not owned, not gated, not behind a paywall.

OpenRoot = going back to the roots of what humans need, and making it open to all.

### The Big Picture

I'm one guy with a phone, building something that could change how communities work. Not because I want to get rich — the system is designed to be profitable — but because the way we're currently living is unsustainable. We pay companies for things we could produce ourselves. We throw away materials we could build with. We depend on systems we don't control.

OpenRoot is about taking that control back — together, as a community, from the ground up.
