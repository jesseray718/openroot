# Safety Boundary

| Hazard | Control | Required verification |
|---|---|---|
| Replace | Replace | Replace |

Stop work and seek qualified review for structural loads, occupied structures, pressurized systems, steam, high temperature, hazardous electricity, potable water, wastewater, food, combustion, fuels, toxic materials, or other safety-critical work.

Do not call this design safe, certified, compliant, field-ready, or proven without documented evidence and appropriate review.
