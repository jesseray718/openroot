# Greenhouse Air Gap Specification

**Requirement**: Maintain a continuous air gap between the black solar-absorber surface of the Aero-Disc and the clear outer membrane.

- Gap height: 25–35 mm (recommended starting value 30 mm)
- Formed by discrete spacers around the perimeter
- Clear membrane registered to the outer cylindrical wall

Purpose:
1. Greenhouse effect raises absorber surface temperature
2. Creates an additional buoyancy-driven air path
3. Protects the open-cell face while allowing elevated absorber temperature

Once measured temperatures exist, the sizing script will be updated with a simple parallel buoyancy term. Until then the 5.6 Pa stack figure remains a conservative lower bound.
