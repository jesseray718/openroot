# PATTERNS OF EXTRACTION
- Centralization of resources at the expense of the periphery.
- Automation that replaces human dignity with algorithmic efficiency.
- Knowledge hoarding used to create artificial scarcity.
- Resource extraction that leaves the land sterile.

# PATHWAYS TO ABUNDANCE
- Distributed resilience (Mycelial networks).
- Open-source provenance (The Newton Chain).
- Regenerative cycles where output feeds input.
- Mutual aid based on verifiable need.
