## Purpose

The Advancement Engine is the machine that answers one question:

> What is the single next thing that will furthest advance the human species as a whole, measured in useful joules?

It watches the thermodynamic ledger, finds the bottlenecks, and proposes the highest-leverage **STEP**.

Anyone can fund a STEP.  
Anyone can solve a STEP.  
The solution must be open-source and must produce a measured η gain.

This replaces consumer culture’s “what will sell” with “what will reduce future human work the most.”

---

## The Tokens Involved

- **STEP** — minted to the solver when the need is met and η is proven.
- **SKILL** — minted to the people who actually build, install, measure, and validate the physical solution.
- **WORK** — the base ledger unit. Ongoing work savings from the solution continue to mint small amounts of WORK over time.
- **ACRE** — the permanent innovation token. A successful STEP automatically qualifies for (and can multiply) ACRE.

All four share the same thermodynamic ledger.

---

## How a STEP Works

1. The Engine (or any person) proposes a STEP: clear need + success criteria + expected η gain.
2. Anyone contributes any amount in any currency into transparent escrow.
3. First solver publishes open plans, data, and measured thermodynamic proof.
4. Two independent validators confirm the η delta.
5. Escrow releases to the solver.
6. STEP is minted.
7. People who execute the physical work earn SKILL.
8. The η record is written permanently. Future work savings continue to generate WORK that can flow back to the original contributors.

---

## Value Source (Plain Language)

The value does not come from the coin itself.  

The value comes from the fact that the solution permanently removes human joules from future work.

If a better method or a better machine means the next ten thousand builds take 40 % less human time, that saved work is real wealth. The ledger measures it and can return a small percentage of those savings as WORK tokens over time. That is the “affiliate” stream.

Because every successful STEP raises system η, the Engine becomes better at finding the next bottleneck. The whole system compounds.
