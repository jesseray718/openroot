# Lightest Practical Heating Methods (ranked by mass efficiency)

1. Passive solar + selective absorbers (preferred)
   - High absorptivity inner surfaces
   - Low-emissivity outer coatings
   - Greenhouse effect does most of the work

2. Low-power microwave / RF heating of a small central absorber
   - Only the core is heated; convection distributes
   - Extremely mass-efficient when power is available

3. Phase-change or chemical heat storage (night / eclipse buffer only)

Avoid pure resistive heaters for the primary system — they are mass-heavy.
