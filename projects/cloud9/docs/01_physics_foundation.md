# Physics Foundation — Cloud Nine Principle

A geodesic / tensegrity sphere can float in atmosphere when the average density of (shell + contents) is lower than the surrounding air.

Key relation:
- Shell mass scales roughly with surface area (\~ r²)
- Buoyant force scales with volume (\~ r³)

Classic order-of-magnitude result (Fuller / Bowers):
At \~400 m radius the enclosed air mass is already \~1000× the structural mass of a light geodesic shell. Heating the internal air by only 1 °C produces enough density reduction for flotation.

Practical implications:
- Larger is easier (better surface-to-volume)
- Extremely light shell is mandatory
- Passive solar heating is the highest-η first approach
