# Simulation: Redirecting Global Military Expenditure under Agape Priority

## Starting Point (2025)
- Global military expenditure: $2.887 trillion (SIPRI)
- Effective deployable surplus after 60% realization factor: \~$1.73 trillion per year
- Priority rule: always raise the current lowest nodes first

## Projected Sequence
Year 1–3: Collapse of extreme material deprivation (nutrition, water, basic health, shelter)
Year 4–10: New floor — secure housing, local food systems, primary tools and education
Year 11–25: Capability layer — local productive capacity, open knowledge, decentralized infrastructure
Longer horizon: Unnecessary suffering from material exclusion approaches zero; abundance has no artificial ceiling

## Core Result
The same annual resource volume currently spent on military establishments is, under strict lowest-node-first allocation, sufficient to raise the global material floor high enough that the most severe forms of avoidable suffering become rare within roughly one generation. After that point the system compounds into shared capability rather than relative dominance.
