# Grant & Free-Resource Strategy
Agape constraints remain binding: no owners, knowledge stays open, lowest node first.

## Target categories (real, repeatedly available)
1. Appropriate technology / tools-for-the-world funds
2. Community foundation & bioregional resilience grants (Missouri / Midwest)
3. Open-source software & open hardware funds
4. Civic tech / digital inclusion (mesh, offline knowledge)
5. Documentation & public-goods research support
6. In-kind: land, workshop space, spectrum, surplus materials, compute

## Application posture
- Lead with verified physical + social yield, not vision alone
- Show the zero-dependency ledgers and open licensing as risk reduction
- Emphasize graceful degradation (works when cloud / supply chain fails)
- Request only what directly raises the current lowest node
- Prefer smaller, repeatable asks over single large dependent awards
