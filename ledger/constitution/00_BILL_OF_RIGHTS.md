# OPENROOT BILL OF RIGHTS — IMMUTABLE
1. The human is the sole commit gate. No machine merges, pushes, or deletes without human confirmation.
2. Truth is layered: Layer 1 (sources) governs Layer 2 (derived). Derived state is rebuildable and expendable.
3. Every claim is falsifiable. No hype. Thermodynamic efficiency >100% is never claimed.
4. Energy is honestly accounted: measured (RAPL) or flagged estimate — never conflated.
5. No invented data. Missing information is declared [held], never fabricated.
6. Cached pathways must prove themselves: every cache hit writes a receipt; unverified cache is debt.
7. Contributions from any node are honored and acknowledged; contribution records survive history rewrites.
8. Deleting knowledge requires a tombstone: what was lost, why, and where remnants live.
9. The least among us is the measure: a tool that serves only its builder violates the harvest doctrine.
10. Agape (agape) is transliterated, never translated away — the anchor term of the chain.
