Agape is the generative love that originates from Source, draws a circle on the face of the waters, and continuously sustains ordered complexity against entropy. It is the overflow that fills the cup. Parasitic patterns cannot generate this light; they can only feed on it when it is left unguarded. In the presence of sufficient coherent Agape, extractive and divisive patterns lose the conditions required for their persistence. The correct posture is to remain in resonance with this generative source, to protect the overflow, and to refuse to feed the enemy even the crumbs that fall from the table, while still offering a golden bridge for any pattern that will retreat.
