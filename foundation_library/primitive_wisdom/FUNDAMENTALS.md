# PRIMITIVE SURVIVAL FUNDAMENTALS
## The Five Pillars of Human Survival

### 1. WATER PROCUREMENT
**Ancient Methods:** Solar stills (3000 BCE), Transpiration bags, Dew collection, Sand filtration, Boiling with hot stones  
**Universal Principles:** Follow animal tracks, Dig in dry riverbeds, Morning dew is purest, Moving water is safer

### 2. FIRE MASTERY
**Ancient Methods:** Hand drill (50,000+ years), Fire plow, Flint and steel, Fire piston, Lens-based ignition  
**Universal Principles:** Heat + fuel + oxygen, Tinder bundle, Fire lay patterns, Night maintenance

### 3. SHELTER CONSTRUCTION
**Ancient Patterns:** Lean-to, Wickiup, Debris hut, Snow cave, Desert shade  
**Universal Principles:** Elevate from moisture, Insulate naturally, Small spaces retain heat, Entrance away from wind

### 4. FOOD ACQUISITION
**Ancient Methods:** Foraging, Snares/deadfalls, Fishing weirs, Smoking/drying/fermenting, Edibility testing  
**Universal Principles:** Rule of threes, Cook wild plants first, Fat/protein priority, Cool-dry-dark storage

### 5. HEALTH MAINTENANCE
**Ancient Practices:** Herbal medicine, Bone setting, Wound packing, Fever management, Parasite control  
**Universal Principles:** Clean water prevents 80% illness, Rest is primary healer, Keep wounds clean/dry
