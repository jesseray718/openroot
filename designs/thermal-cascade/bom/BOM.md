# Bill of Materials — OpenRoot Thermal Cascade

`BOM.csv` is the canonical machine-readable BOM.

Every BOM reference must link to a drawing, source file, assembly instruction, or test requirement. Record dimensions, material grade, rating, alternatives, and criticality. A supplier link or current price is useful but must never be the only identity for a component.
