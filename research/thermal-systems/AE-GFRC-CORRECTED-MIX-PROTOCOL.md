# AE-GFRC Corrected Mix Protocol
**UNE:** DV.GEN.TH.AE01.CMP
**Version:** 1.0 (Corrected)
**Date:** 2026-07-09

## Overview
This document corrects the AE-GFRC mix protocol based on validated specifications and addresses the issues identified in the previous version.

## Corrected Material Ratios
- Cement (Type I/II): 1 part
- Sand (washed, fine): 0.8 parts
- Glass fiber (alkali-resistant): 0.04 parts by volume
- Xanthan gum: 0.2% by cement weight
- Activated carbon: 3-5% by cement weight
- Water: 0.33 L/kg cement (starting point — tune for workability)

## Corrected Mixing Sequence

### Step 1: Dry Components Preparation
1. Weigh cement accurately
2. Add activated carbon (3-5% by cement weight)
3. Mix thoroughly until uniform color
4. Add glass fibers gradually while mixing
5. Ensure even distribution of fibers

### Step 2: Liquid Components Preparation
1. Measure required water (0.33 L/kg cement)
2. Add xanthan gum (0.2% by cement weight) to water
3. Mix until xanthan gum is fully dissolved
4. Add any additional liquid additives

### Step 3: Foaming Process
1. Add foaming agent (type and dosage TBD — protein-based recommended, ~0.1-0.3% by cement weight)
2. Mix at consistent speed (300-500 RPM)
3. Continue until desired foam consistency achieved
4. Monitor foam stability

### Step 4: Final Mix
1. Gradually add liquid mixture to dry components
2. Mix thoroughly for 5-7 minutes
3. Ensure no dry pockets remain
4. Check consistency - should be pourable but hold shape

## Corrected Curing Protocol

### 21-Day Wet Cure Schedule
1. **Days 1-7:** Keep mold covered and moist
2. **Days 8-14:** Controlled drying phase
3. **Days 15-21:** Ambient cure
4. **Daily:** Log temperature and humidity

### Quality Control Checkpoints
- Day 3: Initial demold and documentation
- Day 7: Mid-cure documentation
- Day 14: Pre-final documentation
- Day 21: Final demold and comprehensive documentation

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Foam Stability Issues
- Increase xanthan gum percentage
- Reduce aeration agent quantity
- Mix at lower speed
- Retry with adjusted parameters

### Thermal Performance Issues
- Increase activated carbon percentage
- Verify proper mixing and distribution
- Check for surface contamination
- Consider alternative absorption enhancers

### Structural Issues
- Adjust fiber content and distribution
- Verify proper curing conditions
- Check for proper mixing and consolidation
- Consider adding additional reinforcement
