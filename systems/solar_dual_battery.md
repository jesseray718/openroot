# Solar Dual-Battery Thermal System (Aero-Disc Version)

Single-person passive thermal system optimized for maximum energy capture and storage.

## Architecture
Hot humid air → Desiccant → Underground volumetric open-cell aerocement labyrinth → Copper coil in Cold ferrocement tank (with high-ε sky radiator lid) → Aero-disc (selective absorber + vortex/chimney) → Copper coil in Hot ferrocement tank → Ambient air exits.

ΔT between tanks drives Stirling → flywheel → clutchable belt drive → alternator + TEGs.

## Optimized Parameters (26 m²)
- Aero-disc: 26 m² high-performance selective absorber (≥80%)
- Labyrinth: 0.6–0.8 m³ open-cell aerocement @ 120–200 m²/m³
- Cold tank: 3.5–4 m³ + 16–20 m² sky lid
- Hot tank: 3.5–4 m³
- Coil UA: 400–550 W/K each

## Daily Output (clear sky)
Heat 10–13 kWh + Cold 5–8 kWh = 15–21 kWh/day
