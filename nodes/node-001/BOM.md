# Node-001 — Chicken-wire / Park-bench Prototype
# Lowest-joule physical entry point for OpenRoot

## Goal
Produce one measurable passive ΔT + optional local mesh hop.
This is the atomic unit. Everything larger is fractal copies of this.

## Materials (approximate, local / scrap preferred)
- Chicken wire or light welded wire mesh : 1 piece ≈ 1.5 m × 1.0 m
- Light frame stock (rebar, scrap wood, bamboo, or PVC) : enough for a simple rectangular or A-frame base ≈ 1.2 m long × 0.6 m wide × 0.4–0.6 m high
- High-emissivity surface material for the top / sky-facing face:
  - Option A: any flat dark or matte surface already on hand
  - Option B: early open-cell aerocement panel or charcoal-infused open-cell material
  - Option C: aluminum foil (shiny side down) + thin black paint or high-ε coating on the sky side
- Ground contact: direct earth, or a shallow tray of soil / sand / wet open-cell material
- Fasteners: wire, zip ties, or screws already available
- Two temperature sensors (any): cheap thermistors, DS18B20, or even the phone itself + one external probe
- Optional: small mesh radio or just a phone for logging

## Cut / Build List
1. Cut or bend frame to roughly park-bench scale (does not need to be exact).
2. Stretch chicken wire over the frame so it has a clear upward-facing surface.
3. Attach the high-emissivity surface to the top so it can see the open sky.
4. Place the base in solid contact with the ground.
5. Mount one temperature sensor on the sky-facing surface.
6. Mount the second temperature sensor in free air nearby (same height, shaded from direct sun).
7. Record both temperatures every 5–15 minutes on a clear night.

## Measurement Protocol
- Start after sunset on a clear or mostly clear night.
- Log: timestamp, surface temperature, air temperature, notes (clouds, wind, humidity if known).
- Continue at least 2–4 hours.
- Calculate simple ΔT = air_temp − surface_temp (positive = surface is cooler = useful radiative cooling).

## Success Criteria (minimum)
- Any consistent positive nighttime ΔT is a win.
- Numbers written into the seed = the node is real.
- Photos + the numbers become the open template for the next 63 nodes.

## Notes
This node is deliberately ugly and cheap.
It is the earthly training set for the same geometry that works against the 3 K sink in space.
Do not wait for perfect materials. Start with what is already in reach.
