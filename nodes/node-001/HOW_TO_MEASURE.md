# How to take the first measurement

1. Build the simple frame from the BOM (even partial is fine).
2. On a clear night after sunset, place the sky-facing surface so it can see the open sky.
3. Run:

   bash $HOME/openroot/bin/or_log_dt

4. Enter the two temperatures when prompted.
5. The numbers are automatically written into the seed and into the node log.

That is the entire first scientific act.
