# Why this exists (plain language)

This project exists so that useful physical work and free communication can reach the people who need it most, with the least possible ongoing cost and effort.

It starts with small, almost free physical units (chicken-wire frames, simple radiative surfaces, ground coupling). These units create real temperature differences and real local connectivity without continuous electricity.

The same pattern scales. Twelve main points in the sky, then more points filling in between them, then more, until the whole surface of the Earth (and later beyond) can talk to itself without depending on any single company or government.

The full planetary version was calculated to cost about forty seconds of what the world currently spends on telecommunications, or about two cents per person alive. After that, upkeep is a tiny fraction.

Anyone can help. You do not have to understand every layer. If you make one of the small physical units better, cheaper, longer-lasting, or easier to build, you are helping. If you measure a real temperature difference and share the numbers, you are helping. If you improve the offline tools, you are helping.

The project is designed so that it does not die if any one person disappears. The knowledge is open, the designs are free to copy, and the goal is simple: raise the floor for the people who currently have the least.

That is the entire point.
