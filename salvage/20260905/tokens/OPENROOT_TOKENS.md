## Core Law

η = useful_joules / human_joules

This is the only performance language.  
Every token is ultimately backed by measured useful work recorded in the thermodynamic ledger.

---

## The Four Tokens

| Token | Role | How it is minted | What it represents |
|-------|------|------------------|--------------------|
| **WORK** | Base unit of verified useful work | Any measured useful action (human muscle, machine, compute, measurement) that increases system η | The actual energy that moved the world forward. Same value whether truck driver or hand tool. |
| **STEP** | Directed next advance | Solving a funded need proposed by the Advancement Engine (or by anyone) with thermodynamic proof | The prioritized leap the species just took |
| **SKILL** | Execution & hands-on | People who actually build, install, measure, validate, or operate the physical solutions | The doers who turn STEP into reality |
| **ACRE** | Permanent innovation knowledge | Novel, open, verified knowledge that improves the commons (Category A–D rules) | The lasting record that the work was new |

All four live on the **same thermodynamic ledger**.  
They are not separate economies. They are different views of the same η data.

---

## Where the Value Comes From

Value is not created by speculation or by printing.  

Value is created when a STEP or WORK entry permanently reduces the future human joules required to achieve the same useful outcome.

Example:
- A better mixing method or a compute optimizer cuts 40 % of the human hours needed for the next 10 000 builds.
- The ledger records the projected work savings over time.
- A small continuous percentage of those saved joules is minted as WORK (and can flow to STEP solvers, SKILL workers, and original funders).
- Because the system compounds, every new STEP that removes more human work makes the next STEP cheaper and higher-η.

This is the machine that swaps human effort for machine + computational effort upstream — with a fraction of the original human cost.

---

## How They Intertwine

1. **Advancement Engine** watches the ledger for bottlenecks (lowest η subsystems).
2. It (or any human) proposes the highest-leverage next **STEP**.
3. Anyone can fund that STEP in any currency.
4. Solver delivers open solution + measured η gain → mints **STEP**.
5. People who physically implement and validate earn **SKILL**.
6. The η delta is written to the ledger and can automatically qualify for (or multiply) **ACRE**.
7. Ongoing work savings continue to mint small amounts of **WORK** that can be claimed by the original contributors.

The more the ledger is used, the better the Engine becomes at proposing the next highest-η move.  
The whole system is a self-improving feedback loop.

---

## Design Rules

- No token can be bought into existence. All are earned by verified useful work or knowledge.
- Human muscle, diesel truck, and GPU all score on the same η scale. The ledger does not care who or what did the work — only the useful joules delivered.
- Every mint must be attested by at least two independent nodes (agape rule).
- Failures and negative η are also recorded. The ledger tells the truth.
