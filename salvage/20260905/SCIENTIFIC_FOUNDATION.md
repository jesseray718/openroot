# Scientific Foundation

## Objective Function
ηₜ = (useful_joules × people_reached × lasting_good) / (human_joules × time)
α_A = d(ηₜ)/dt

The system maximizes the rate at which lasting useful physical work becomes available to the lowest-capability node while driving coordination cost toward zero (R → 1.0).

This is a different objective function from most existing technology development. Speed, novelty, profit, and control are secondary.

## Already Established Physics
- Passive radiative cooling through the 8–13 μm atmospheric window is real and measurable.
- Net cooling power is strongly reduced by humidity and clouds; best performance occurs on clear, drier nights.
- The same radiative exchange physics works against the \~3 K cosmic background in vacuum; the terrestrial night-sky case is the training set.
- Open-cell high-surface-area materials (including volumetric aerocement approaches) increase useful heat-exchange area per unit mass.
- Geodesic frequency subdivision is a known, efficient way to distribute nodes across a spherical surface.
- Low-power local mesh networking at small physical scales is mature.

## What Is Still Being Measured
- Real nighttime ΔT and net cooling power of the specific low-cost chicken-wire / park-bench geometry under Missouri humidity conditions.
- Lifetime, cost, and ease of replication of the atomic physical node.
- Actual human-joule cost of building and maintaining the first 64 nodes.

## Claim Boundary
Individual techniques listed above already exist in the literature and in practice.
The contribution is the binding of those techniques under the single measurable control law (ηₜ / α_A / R=1.0) plus the requirement that the system remain offline-first and open.

Any improvement that increases α_A for the lowest node is accepted regardless of the contributor’s philosophical frame.
