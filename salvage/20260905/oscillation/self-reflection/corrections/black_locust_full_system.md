# Black Locust — Full System Role (Locked 2026-08-11)

## Core properties
- Fastest growing tree on Earth (bamboo is a grass)
- Living roots remain and continue sequestering carbon while shoots are harvested
- Coppice produces straight shoots: easy to cut, bundle, haul
- No seasoning required, no chopping required, ready to burn
- Approximately 1% of the human effort of traditional firewood harvest
- Extremely hard, rot-resistant (excellent untreated ground contact)
- One of the hottest-burning woods
- Exceptional honey / nectar source for bees

## Production modes
1. Continuous fuel via coppice (outer rings)
2. Beams and stick-building / green-stick construction material
3. Full-grown trees managed for bloom + apiary (core sanctuary)

## Civilizational role
- Carbon-negative energy source
- Reforestation + aquifer restoration simultaneously
- Replaces fragile, extractive wood systems that historically contributed to civilizational collapse
- Forms an anti-fragile, regenerative backbone that any person can participate in

## Integration with the thermal cascade
Black Locust → Rocket Mass Heater → thermal cascade / atmospheric energy harvester → radiative exchange with the \~3 K cosmic sink.

The same updraft that drives the rocket mass heater also moves air through underground / evaporative cooling labyrinths, creating both heat storage and cold storage. Heat is the appropriate long-term storage medium for excess energy in this architecture.
