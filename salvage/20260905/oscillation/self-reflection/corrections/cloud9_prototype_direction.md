# Cloud Nine Prototype Direction (Locked 2026-08-11)

## Core concept
Tensegrity structure with vacuum-gapped thermal architecture.

## Key elements
- Inner black absorber for maximum solar radiance capture
- Vacuum gap for thermal isolation
- Outer surface optimized to radiate toward the \~3 K cosmic background
- As the structure gains altitude, ambient temperature falls and ΔT increases
- Optional microwave magnetron backup for periods without sun
- Gyroscopic orientation system to track the sun
- Capacity for dirt + plants (floating food production)
- Soft-landing fallback: if primary systems fail, gyroscopic / autorotation behavior (helicopter-seed principle) allows controlled return

## Energy principle
Temperature differential (ΔT) is the core free-energy resource.
The system is designed to maximize continuous ΔT between the hot inner chamber and the cold outer radiative surface / cosmic sink, with component ratios optimized to avoid bottlenecks in energy flow.

- Builds on the three oscillation cycles (TinyGS, Meshtastic, MeshCore) as communication / mesh layers
- Intended to become the intergalactic-scale expression of the same physics proven at chicken-wire scale
