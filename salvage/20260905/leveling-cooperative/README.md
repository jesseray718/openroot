# OpenRoot Leveling Cooperative

**Autonomous playing-field leveling for personal credit & wealth**

No owners. No corporate extraction.  
Members invest capital (not their full credit identity).  
The pool compounds.  
Dividends return to members.  
A fixed percentage of surplus flows automatically to those who need the hand-up most.

Governed by the same ledger technology used in ACRE and the thermodynamic records.  
Decision coefficients calculated by a lightweight nanobot hive (pure local rules).
