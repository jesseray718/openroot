# MOLD CONSTRUCTION PLAN: 1-METER SPIRAL COLUMN
**Goal:** Build a mold to cast the Ultra-Tight Spiral Aerocement.
**Materials Needed:** PVC Pipe, Wood, Silicone, Polycarbonate/Clear Plastic

---

## 1. OPTION A: PVC PIPE MOLD (Recommended)
**If you have a 4-inch PVC pipe (12 inches long):**

### Build Steps:
1.  **Cut the Pipe:** Cut a 12-inch (30 cm) section of 4-inch PVC pipe.
2.  **Create the Base:** Cut a circle of plywood or thick plastic to fit the bottom of the pipe. Glue it with silicone.
3.  **Create the Spiral Guide:**
    *   Take a strip of wood or stiff plastic (1/2 inch wide, 3mm thick).
    *   Wrap it around the INSIDE of the pipe in a spiral.
    *   **Pitch:** Every 9.7 cm (4 inches) the spiral should complete one full turn.
    *   Secure with silicone.
4.  **Top Cover:** Cut a clear polycarbonate sheet to fit the top. Drill holes for sensors.
5.  **Seal:** Use silicone to seal all joints.

### Pros:
- Easy to find at hardware store
- Smooth surface for easy removal
- Reusable

### Cons:
- Only 12 inches tall (need to stack or use taller pipe)

---

## 2. OPTION B: 5-GALLON BUCKET MOLD (Using Your Supplies)
**If you don't have PVC, use the bucket:**

### Build Steps:
1.  **Clean the Bucket:** Remove all labels and residue.
2.  **Create Inner Form:** Use a smaller container (like a 1-gallon jug) to create the hollow center.
3.  **Spiral Divider:** Cut a long strip of plastic (from a storage bin) and bend it into a spiral inside the bucket.
4.  **Secure:** Use silicone to attach the spiral to the bucket walls.
5.  **Top Cover:** Use clear plastic sheet or polycarbonate.

### Pros:
- You already have the bucket
- Cheap/free

### Cons:
- Harder to remove the cement once cured
- Less precise spiral

---

## 3. OPTION C: CARDBOARD + FIBERGLASS (Emergency Backup)
**If you have no PVC or plastic:**

### Build Steps:
1.  **Roll Cardboard:** Roll thick cardboard into a cylinder (15 cm diameter).
2.  **Reinforce:** Wrap with fiberglass mesh or duct tape.
3.  **Spiral:** Use thin wood strips to create the spiral divider inside.
4.  **Waterproof:** Coat inside with silicone or plastic wrap.

### Pros:
- Uses trash/recycled materials
- Very cheap

### Cons:
- Single use (cardboard dissolves)
- Less durable

---

## 4. SENSOR PLACEMENT
Regardless of mold type, place sensors at:
- **Sensor 1 (Inlet):** Bottom of the column (0 cm)
- **Sensor 2 (Mid):** Middle of the column (50 cm)
- **Sensor 3 (Exit):** Top of the column (100 cm)

If using a 12-inch PVC pipe, scale down:
- **Sensor 1:** Bottom (0 cm)
- **Sensor 2:** Middle (15 cm)
- **Sensor 3:** Top (30 cm)
