# AEROCEMENT MIXING GUIDE: THE "SECRET SAUCE"
**WARNING:** Precision is critical. Do not rush.
**Goal:** Create Open-Cell structure (bubbles must pop when stirred stops).

---

## 1. INGREDIENT RATIOS (For ~5 Liters of Mix)
*Adjust based on how much cement you have. The ratio is **1 part Gel : 2 parts Cement**.*

### A. The Gel (The "Glue")
1.  **Xanthan Gum:** 15 grams (approx. 1 tablespoon).
2.  **Isopropyl Alcohol (70%):** 200 ml (approx. 1 cup).
    *   *Mix Gum into Alcohol FIRST.* Stir vigorously until no clumps remain. This prevents the gum from clumping in water.
3.  **Water:** 800 ml (approx. 3.5 cups).
4.  **Dawn Ultra:** 50-100 ml (approx. 1/4 to 1/2 cup).
    *   *Add Dawn to Water.* Mix gently to create a gel. **Do not whip it into foam yet.**
5.  **Combine:** Pour the Alcohol/Gum mix into the Water/Dawn mix. Stir gently.
6.  **Add Carbon:** Add **20-30g Activated Carbon Powder** to the gel NOW. Stir until the gel is **uniformly BLACK**.
    *   *Why now?* If you add it later, it will lighten the color and not coat the pores evenly.

### B. The Cement Mix
1.  **Cement:** 2 parts (by volume) to your Gel.
    *   *Example:* If you have 1 Liter of Gel, use 2 Liters of Cement.
    *   *Your Supply:* If you have 1/5 of a 5-gallon bucket (~2 gallons / 7.5 Liters) of cement, you will need about **3.75 Liters of Gel**.
2.  **Reinforcement (Optional):** If you have fiberglass strands or shredded plastic, add a handful now.

---

## 2. THE MIXING PROCESS (CRITICAL STEP)
**Equipment:** Drill with a paddle mixer attachment (or a sturdy stick if no drill).
**Speed:** 300 - 500 RPM (Medium speed). **Do not go too fast or you will trap too much air.**

### Step-by-Step:
1.  **Pour Gel:** Put the black gel into a bucket.
2.  **Add Cement:** Slowly pour the cement into the gel while the drill is running.
3.  **The Stir:** Keep mixing.
    *   *Phase 1 (Closed Cell):* The mix will look thick and creamy. Bubbles are trapped inside.
    *   *Phase 2 (The Transition):* Keep stirring. You will see the texture change. It becomes lighter and fluffier.
    *   **THE MOMENT OF TRUTH:** Stop the drill for 2 seconds. Look at the surface.
        *   **If bubbles stay sealed:** Keep stirring.
        *   **If bubbles POP and collapse:** **STOP IMMEDIATELY.**
4.  **Pour:** Pour the mix into your mold **within 30 seconds** of stopping. If you wait, it will start to set and lose the open-cell structure.

---

## 3. CURING
1.  **Cover:** Immediately cover the wet cement with a plastic sheet or damp cloth.
2.  **Moisture:** Keep it moist for **48 hours**. Do not let it dry out in the sun.
3.  **Strength:**
    *   24 Hours: Handle with care (fragile).
    *   7 Days: 50% strength.
    *   28 Days: Full strength.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING
| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Too Dense (No Air)** | Not stirred enough | Stir longer next time until bubbles pop. |
| **Too Weak (Crumbles)** | Over-stirred | Stir less next time. Stop as soon as bubbles pop. |
| **Clumpy Gum** | Gum added to water directly | Always mix gum into alcohol FIRST. |
| **Not Black** | Carbon added too late | Add carbon to gel before mixing with cement. |

---

## 5. YOUR PLAN
1.  **Calculate:** How much cement do you have? (Estimate volume).
2.  **Scale:** Multiply by 0.5 to get the amount of Gel needed.
3.  **Mix:** Follow the steps above.
4.  **Test:** Pour a small cup first to test the "Bubble Pop" moment before pouring the whole batch.

**End of Guide.**
