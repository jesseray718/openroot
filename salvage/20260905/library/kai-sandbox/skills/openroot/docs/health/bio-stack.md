# OpenRoot Biological Stack (v1.0)

## Integrated Ecosystem Design
Combining permaculture, agroforestry, and aquaculture for self-sustaining production.

### Core Species
| Species | Function | Yield | Timeline |
|---------|----------|-------|----------|
| **Black Locust** | Firewood, construction timber, nitrogen fixation, bee forage | High timber value | 10-15 yr maturity |
| **Bees** | Pollination, honey, wax, royal jelly | Honey harvest annually | Year 1+ |
| **Willow** | Living structures, compost material, erosion control | Immediate sale | 1-2 yr |
| **Tilapia** | Protein, fertilizer (fish waste) | Fish biomass weekly | Year 1 |
| **Red Wiggler Worms** | Composting, fish feed, soil health | Biomass constant | Month 1+ |
| **Duckweed** | Fast-growing protein for fish, fertilizer | Rapid turnover | Week 1+ |
| **Lion's Mane Mushroom** | Medicinal mushroom, food value | High market price | Seasonal |

### Infrastructure Layers
1. **Water Storage:** Ferrocement tanks (aquaponics reservoirs)
2. **Growing Beds:** Aquaponic towers (herbs/medicinals)
3. **Habitat:** Living structures (shade/bees/willow)
4. **Processing:** Freeze drying (preserve harvest for shelf stability)

### Economic Model
- Short Term: Willow structures + consulting ($500-2000/mo)
- Medium Term: Timber thinnings + honey + fish sales ($5k+/mo)
- Long Term: Whole farm operations + processing (Freeze dried goods + timber contracts)
