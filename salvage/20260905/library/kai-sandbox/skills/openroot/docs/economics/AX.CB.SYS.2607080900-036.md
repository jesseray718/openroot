# AX-036 — The Multi-Function Yield Law

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

A credit instrument properly utilized yields simultaneously: payment history, utilization optimization, cashback return, tax-deductible trails (if business), and fraud protection. A debit card yields only the transaction itself. The differential between a credit-routed dollar and a debit-routed dollar is the sum of all secondary yields — this is the credit premium.

## Derivation

Comparison of credit card vs debit card output per transaction:
- Credit card: +1 payment history event, +utilization data point, +cashback (1-3%), +fraud protection (FCBA), +tax trail (if business card)
- Debit card: transaction completed, nothing else

The credit premium = sum of all yields not present in debit routing.

## Application

Evaluate every payment method choice against the credit premium. If a credit instrument is available and the payment can be routed through it, the debit/cash alternative represents foregone yield equal to the credit premium.
