## The Current Extraction Model

Global telecom industry revenue: $1.7 trillion/year
Global households with internet: ~1.8 billion
Average annual spend per household: ~$944/year (global avg) / ~$2,160/year (US avg)

Where the money goes NOW:
- ~60% to 10 mega-corporations (AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, China Mobile, Deutsche Telekom, Telefonica, NTT, Orange, T-Mobile, BT)
- ~20% to infrastructure debt service (bondholders)
- ~10% to government fees/taxes/spectrum auctions
- ~7% to executive compensation and shareholder dividends
- ~3% to actual network maintenance at the local level

**Result: 97% of money collected LEAVES the community. 3% stays.** This is economic extraction by design.

## The Mesh Redirect Model

One-time network investment: $50/household (LoRa node)
Annual maintenance per household: ~$12/year (shared relay costs)
Annual savings per household: $932/year (global avg) / $2,148/year (US avg)

Money STAYS in the community:
- 100% of former bill payments retained locally
- Economic multiplier effect: 4-7x local circulation
- Each dollar saved = $4-7 in community economic activity
- This is "obtain a yield" at civilizational scale

## The Societal Transformation

When $1.7 trillion stops flowing upward to telecom oligarchs and starts circulating in communities:

**Education:** Every child on Earth has access to the same information. No school district is "better funded" because of property tax inequalities. The mesh delivers educational content to every node equally.

**Healthcare:** Telemedicine reaches the most remote village. A doctor in St. Louis consults with a patient in rural Bangladesh through the mesh. Health monitoring IoT devices transmit vitals at zero cost.

**Economic Democracy:** $1.7 trillion/year stops subsidizing yachts for telecom executives and starts funding bakeries, clinics, schools, farms, and workshops in every community on Earth.

**Political Freedom:** No government can cut communication to suppress dissent. No platform can deplatform a movement. No algorithm can shadowban a voice. The network has no center to capture.

**Resilience:** Natural disasters, war, pandemic, economic collapse -- the mesh survives. Community continues communicating, coordinating, and recovering.

**The End of the Digital Divide:** The concept of "developing" vs "developed" nations dissolves when every human has identical access to the network. The only variable becomes physical proximity to a node -- and nodes cost $50.
