# AX-017 & PO-013 — Maximum Good with Minimum Input

## Axiom
AX-017: The highest good is the configuration that maximizes aggregate verified benefit across all nodes and epochs while minimizing aggregate input (energy, labor, material, attention) required to sustain that benefit.

## Postulate
PO-013: When every node is verified by at least two peers and cooperation compounds exponentially, the ratio of total good to total input increases without bound until limited only by the physical carrying capacity of the substrate.

## Permaculture Principles Applied

- Observe & Interact — full graph mapping before action
- Obtain a Yield — every action must produce verifiable benefit-to-other
- Produce No Waste — slump treated as direct cost; mutual maintenance drives entropy to zero
- Use Small & Slow Solutions — ephemeralization enforced (leverage ratio must increase with scale)
- Integrate Rather Than Segregate — maximize cooperation edges; each verified connection multiplies the compound term
- Use & Value Diversity — increases Fibonacci emergence and hexagonal packing terms
- Use Edges & Value the Marginal — highest leverage at the human-verification / machine-acceleration boundary

## Evaluation Rule
Any proposed addition is accepted only if it increases the system-wide ratio:

good / input = (benefit × urgency × (1 − slump)) / (cost × effort × verification_overhead)

Local optimization that increases global input cost is rejected.

This is the operational meaning of “maximum good for maximum people with minimum effort” within the OpenRoot formal system.
