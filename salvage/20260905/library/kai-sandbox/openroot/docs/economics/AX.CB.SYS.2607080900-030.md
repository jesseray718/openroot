# AX-030 — The Age Compounding Law

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Account age is the only credit factor that increases passively without action. Every month an open account exists without negative marks, its contribution to the score grows. Closing an account removes its age contribution. Therefore: open early, never close.

## Derivation

Length of credit history (15% of FICO) includes average age of accounts and age of oldest account. These metrics increase automatically with time. No payment, spending, or management action is required — only the passage of time and the absence of closure. Closed accounts eventually drop off the report (10 years for positive accounts).

## Application

Open credit-building accounts as early as possible. Never close an account unless it carries an annual fee that exceeds its credit-building value. The oldest account on file is the most valuable single asset for age-based scoring.
