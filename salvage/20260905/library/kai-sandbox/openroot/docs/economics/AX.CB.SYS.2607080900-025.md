# AX-025 — The Routing Principle

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Every dollar already being spent is a latent credit-building event. The method of payment determines whether that event registers with the credit system. Debit, cash, and peer-to-peer transfers generate zero credit yield. Credit-routed payments convert existing spending into credit history without increasing expenditure.

## Derivation

Observation of FICO/VantageScore scoring models reveals that only credit-instrumented transactions generate tradeline reporting. Non-credit payment methods (debit, cash, P2P) are invisible to bureau scoring algorithms. Therefore, the act of spending is necessary but insufficient for credit building — the routing channel is the determining variable.

## Application

Convert all recurring payments to credit-routed channels. Update payment methods on every subscription, bill, and recurring expense to a credit instrument that reports to all three bureaus.
