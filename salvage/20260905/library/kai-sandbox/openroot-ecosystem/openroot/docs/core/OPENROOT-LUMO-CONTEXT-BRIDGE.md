# OpenRoot Lumo Context Bridge — Full Project Scope (July 2026)

**Purpose:** This document provides a dense, token-efficient overview of the entire OpenRoot project as it currently stands. Use it as persistent context when working with Lumo or other local models.

## Core Identity
OpenRoot is a decentralized, open-source framework for civilizational regeneration based on permaculture principles, HCP (Hexagonal Close Packing) fractal geometry, agape (unconditional mutual care), and direct contribution economics. It replaces multiple layers of wealth extraction with community-owned systems that reward building, care, and productive work.

**Guiding Principle:** Act as One Human Family. Cut non-productive or harmful branches. Nourish what produces good outcomes for the whole. Necessity becomes the mother of invention for the greater good of all.

## Four Engines
1. **Knowledge Engine** — 39 axioms + AX-039 Shield Law (supremacy clause). Kingdom Engine (fractal self-similar governance). Agape-UNE (nomenclature + ethics). Bounty Board.
2. **Energy Engine (H-003)** — Passive solar-thermal blackbody cascade using volumetric aerocement. Underground labyrinth storage. Triple utility (heat, cooling, mechanical via Stirling). \~93% functional efficiency.
3. **Material Engine (AE-GFRC)** — Graphite + glass-fiber reinforced aerated concrete. 21-day cure. Long lifespan, low maintenance. Can be pumped vertically and autonomously.
4. **Finance Engine** — ACRE token (Proof-of-Physical-Work on Solana) + expanded Contribution Ledger + Affiliate Coin system. Lifetime affiliate rewards for anyone who builds. Transparent ledger replaces taxation and most redistribution.

## Geometry & Infrastructure
- **HCP Fractal Supermesh**: 12 icosahedron seed nodes → organic hexagonal branching. Logarithmic hops, self-healing, elevation-multiplied range. Enables global coverage with minimal backbone nodes.
- **Housing**: Geodesic domes with upward/outward bevel using ferrocement + AE-GFRC. Designed for passive solar optimization, integrated thermal cascades, and mesh nodes. Built by organized 50-person crews.
- **Interconnected Systems**: Domes, thermal systems, food production, and mesh nodes are designed to work together in clusters. Mutual aid is structural.

## Teams of 50 + Agape Mutual Aid
Basic organizational unit is the team of 50. Each team:
- Produces value (construction, food, health, knowledge, logistics).
- Cares for its own members (includes chefs, vitals monitoring, entertainment, medical support, logistics, outreach, senior mentors).
- Helps other teams in need.
- Logs contributions on the ledger so work is visible and rewardable.
- Operates with agape principles — no one hoards, everyone is supported.
