# AX-027 — The Payment History Primacy

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Payment history constitutes 35% of the FICO score — the single largest weighted factor. One late payment erases months of positive history accumulation. Autopay on full balance is non-negotiable. The system rewards consistency above all other variables.

## Derivation

FICO scoring weights: Payment History (35%), Utilization (30%), Length of History (15%), Credit Mix (10%), New Credit (10%). Payment history dominance means no other factor can compensate for a missed payment. Late payments remain on file for 7 years.

## Application

Enable autopay on every credit instrument. Set to full statement balance, not minimum payment. Verify autopay funding source has sufficient balance at all times.
