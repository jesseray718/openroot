# AX-031 — The Cash Invisibility Problem

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Cash transactions leave no digital trace recognizable by credit bureaus. Any payment made in cash is credit-invisible — it cannot be verified, reported, or scored. The conversion of cash flows to digital-routed flows is a prerequisite for credit building on cash-dependent spending patterns.

## Derivation

Credit bureaus ingest data from reporting institutions (banks, credit card issuers, lenders). Cash transactions occur outside this reporting network entirely. No API, file transfer, or data feed carries cash transaction data to bureaus. Cash is functionally nonexistent in the credit scoring universe.

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-001 (Routing Principle)
- PO-CB-009 (Digital Conversion Requirement)

## Application

Identify all cash-based recurring payments in financial life. Convert each to a digital-routed payment (bank transfer, card payment). For informal arrangements (rent paid to family/friends), establish a digital transfer with memo field documentation.
