## Low-Cost Testing Methods (No Lab Needed)

1. **Panel flex test**  
   Support panel at two ends. Stack sandbags or concrete blocks in center until visible cracking. Record weight. Simple, repeatable.

2. **Drop / impact test**  
   Drop a known weight (e.g. 5–10 kg bag) from 1 m onto center of panel. Look for puncture or delamination.

3. **Black locust compression**  
   Cut short post sample. Stand on end and load with known weight or vehicle (controlled). Measure crush point.

4. **Outdoor exposure**  
   Leave one panel and one timber joint outside for 90 days. Photograph monthly. Check for fiber pull-out, rust, or rot.

5. **Joinery pull test**  
   Assemble one timber-to-panel connection. Pull with come-along or vehicle until failure. Document mode.

All tests cost almost nothing beyond the prototype materials and a phone camera + notebook.
