# Tensegrity Shell with Internal Windows

Primary structure: tensegrity (continuous tension + discontinuous compression).
Lowest mass for given stiffness and resilience.

Outer skin options (ranked by current practicality):
1. Multi-layer ETFE cushions (transparent, extremely light, proven in architecture)
2. Aerogel-filled composite panels
3. Thin glass-ceramic or polycarbonate only where higher optical quality is required

Windows:
- Must be structural (carry load), not holes
- Prefer triangular / hexagonal panels that follow the geodesic frequency
- Look both outward and inward

Open-source tools:
- NASA Tensegrity Robotics Toolkit (NTRT)
- MOTES, TsgFEM, OriTsgEFA (GitHub)
