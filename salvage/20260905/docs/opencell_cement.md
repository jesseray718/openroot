# Opencell Cement Stabilizer

**Problem:** Aircrete collapses when air bubbles pop during curing.

**Solution:** Thixotropic surfactant gel prevents collapse while allowing bubble stabilization.

**Recipe:**
- 1 part thixotropic gel (xanthan gum + Dawn Ultra + water)
- 2 parts pure Portland cement
- Mix while agitating air continuously

**Critical observation:** At maximum air entrainment the bubbles pop. Normal aircrete collapses. With the gel the matrix holds geometry after the air phase is gone. This is a distinct open-cell state.

**Applications:** Insulation, lightweight structural, underground energy matrices, ferrocement composites.

**Status:** Prototype observed. Needs compressive strength, thermal conductivity, porosity distribution data.
