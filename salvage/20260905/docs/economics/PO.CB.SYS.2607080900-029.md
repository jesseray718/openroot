# PO-029 — The Business Vehicle Deduction Loop

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

A vehicle purchased through a business auto loan under the EIN generates: business credit (loan payments reported), tax deduction (depreciation or Section 179), mileage deduction (67¢/mile 2026), and business expense categorization (insurance, fuel, maintenance). All four yields originate from one asset. The vehicle is simultaneously transportation, credit-building instrument, and tax shield.

## Derivation

Business auto loan → payments report to D&B/Experian Business → PAYDEX contribution. Section 179 → up to $100K+ immediate write-off. Mileage → 67¢/mile standard deduction (2026). Insurance/fuel/maintenance → deductible business expenses. Single asset, four independent yield streams.
