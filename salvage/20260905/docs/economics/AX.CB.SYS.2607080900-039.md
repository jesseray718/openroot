# AX-039 — The Tax-Trail Convergence

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Business expenses routed through business credit instruments simultaneously build business credit AND create IRS-defensible deduction trails. Expenses paid in cash or mixed with personal funds lose deductibility defensibility. The credit instrument is also the accounting instrument.

## Derivation

IRS audit defense requires contemporaneous records showing: (1) business purpose, (2) amount, (3) date, (4) payee. Credit card statements provide all four automatically. Cash receipts can be lost, altered, or deemed insufficient. Business credit card statements are the strongest form of expense documentation short of formal invoices.

## Application

Route every business expense through a business credit card. The card statement becomes the primary tax record. Categorize expenses monthly. This satisfies both credit-building and tax-documentation requirements simultaneously.
