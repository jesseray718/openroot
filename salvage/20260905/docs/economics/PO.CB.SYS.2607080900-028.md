# PO-028 — The Digital Conversion Requirement

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Any cash-based recurring payment (rent, side work, personal loans) must be converted to a digital-routed payment (bank transfer, card payment) before it can contribute to credit building. The conversion is a one-time setup cost with perpetual yield thereafter. Unconverted cash flows represent permanent credit yield leakage.

## Derivation

By AX-CB-007, cash is invisible to credit bureaus. By AX-CB-001, only credit-routed payments generate yield. Therefore, any cash payment is a foregone credit event. The conversion cost (setting up a transfer, updating payment method) is incurred once. The yield (monthly credit reporting) accrues perpetually. The ROI of conversion approaches infinity over time.

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-001 (Routing Principle)
- AX-CB-007 (Cash Invisibility Problem)

## Application

Audit all recurring payments for cash usage. For each: (1) identify a digital routing channel, (2) set up the transfer/card payment, (3) document with memo field. One-time cost, perpetual yield.
