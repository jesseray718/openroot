# PO-025 — The Property Self-Payment Principle

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

A property purchased under an LLC using a DSCR loan is qualified on the property's cash flow, not the owner's personal income. If the property generates income (rental, permaculture yield, agricultural production) exceeding the debt service, the property pays its own mortgage while simultaneously building business credit through loan payment history. The asset is self-funding and self-building.

## Derivation

DSCR = Net Operating Income / Debt Service. If DSCR > 1.0, the property generates more income than the loan costs. Lenders qualify based on this ratio, not personal DTI. The LLC holds the mortgage, payments report to business credit, and the property's income services the debt. This creates a closed loop: asset generates income → income pays debt → debt payment builds credit → stronger credit enables next property.
