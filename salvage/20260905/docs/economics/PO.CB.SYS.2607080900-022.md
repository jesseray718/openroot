# PO-022 — The PAYDEX Acceleration Window

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Three to five Net-30 vendor accounts paid early (within 10 days) for 90 consecutive days will generate a D&B PAYDEX score of 80 (maximum). This is achievable within one quarter of initial business credit establishment — faster than any personal credit milestone.

## Derivation

PAYDEX scoring measures payment timeliness on a 0-100 scale. Early payment (before terms) scores 80. Net-30 terms paid within 10 days = early. Three reporting vendors is the minimum for a stable PAYDEX. 90 days establishes sufficient payment pattern for D&B to generate the score. No personal credit milestone (6 months aging, year-old accounts) can be reached as quickly.

## Application

Month 2-4: Open Uline, Grainger, Quill, Crown Office Supplies, Shell Fleet. Buy only what is needed. Pay every invoice within 10 days of receipt (not 30). By Month 4-5, PAYDEX should register at 80.
