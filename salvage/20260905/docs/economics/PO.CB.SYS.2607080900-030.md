# PO-030 — The Imperfect Utilization Superiority

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

An account at 60% utilization with perfect payment history is superior to no account at all. The payment history yield (35% of score) outweighs the utilization penalty (30% of score) on net. Waiting for ideal conditions to open an account delays age compounding (AX-CB-006), which is irrecoverable. The optimal time to open a credit-building account is immediately, regardless of utilization constraints.

## Derivation

Net yield of 60% utilization account = +35% (payment history) - partial penalty on 30% (utilization) = net positive. Net yield of no account = 0. Additionally, age compounding (AX-CB-006) begins at account opening and cannot be recovered retroactively. Every month of delay is permanently lost age accrual.

## Application

If cash constraints prevent maintaining ideal utilization, open the account anyway. 60% utilization + on-time payments > no account. Optimize utilization later when cash flow permits. Age is accruing from day one regardless of utilization.
