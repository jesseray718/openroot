# Voluntary Ledger-Led Self-Governance

Transparent, hash-chained energy/activity ledger (ACRE class) as the sole arbiter.

- Contributions measured in joules (objective)
- Decisions weighted by verified contribution
- No central authority
- Participation voluntary; exit always open

Rooted in measured work, not opinion. Waste becomes visible the moment it is unaccounted.

**Status:** Early conceptual.
