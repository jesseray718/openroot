# Open & Regenerative Tokenomics Seed
Focus: designs that measure real work, avoid extraction, and can run with minimal infrastructure.

Priority:
1. Proof-of-Physical-Work / Proof-of-Useful-Work primitives
2. Quadratic / conviction voting and anti-whale mechanisms
3. Mutual credit & local exchange systems
4. Reputation + attestation systems that do not require heavy chain
5. ACRE-aligned models (mint only on verified physical yield)
6. Historical mutual aid & rotating savings designs that predate blockchain

Cross-reference with:
- OpenRoot ACRE rules
- Leveling-cooperative hand-up logic
- Agape attestation (benefit measured at recipient)

Avoid: pure speculation designs, high-energy PoW, and systems that require constant online connectivity.
