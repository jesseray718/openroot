# Scenario 02 — Water System Failure / Contamination

Trigger: No pressure, boil advisory, known contamination, or source loss.
Immediate:
- Stop consumption of suspect water
- Inventory clean stored water
- Protect remaining clean water from contamination
- Prioritize drinking + critical hygiene for lowest nodes first

Next actions:
- Known local alternative sources (mapped in SE Missouri seed when filled)
- Treatment hierarchy: heat, filtration, chemical (with correct contact time)
- Greywater discipline
- Sanitation changes to protect remaining clean water

Failure modes: using contaminated water for tooth-brushing/cooking, over-allocating to non-critical uses, failing to measure treatment.
