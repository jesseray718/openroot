# Survival Scenarios — Real-Life Problem Resolver Seed
Purpose: give a mesh node the ability to answer “What do I do right now?” under natural disaster or conflict conditions using only local knowledge and resources.

Structure each scenario as:
- Trigger conditions (observable)
- Immediate life-safety actions (first 60 minutes)
- Next 24 hours
- Next 7–30 days
- Resource map (what is usually available vs what must be improvised)
- Common failure modes
- Lowest-node considerations (children, elderly, injured, isolated people)

Priority real scenarios:
1. Extended power outage (days to weeks)
2. Water system failure / contamination
3. Extreme heat or extreme cold
4. Flood / rapid water rise
5. High wind / structural damage
6. Supply chain break (food & medicine)
7. Communication blackout
8. Civil unrest / movement restriction
9. Medical access loss
10. Combined cascading failures

Design rule:
Every recommendation must be executable with tools and materials that can reasonably be present in a household, small community, or vehicle. Prefer techniques that also appear in the Permies, Hackaday, and ancient-wisdom seeds so the knowledge compounds.
