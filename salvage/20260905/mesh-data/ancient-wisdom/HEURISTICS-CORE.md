# Core Heuristics (compressed decision rules)

- Benefit is real only when it lands on someone else (Agape)
- First stabilize the weakest node; strength compounds afterward
- Store in times of plenty the things that keep life in times of scarcity
- Prefer the solution that still works when the complex layer fails
- Measure twice: once for the strong, once for the one who cannot recover easily
- Small, repeated yields beat rare perfect yields
- The edge (margin) often holds the unused opportunity
- Do not consume the seed of next season’s food
- Mutual aid is cheaper than later rescue
- When information is scarce, protect options and the vulnerable first
