# Southeast Missouri Bioregion Identification Seed
Focus: plants, trees, and common wildlife actually present in the Bootheel / SE Missouri / Crowley’s Ridge / adjacent Ozark edge.

Priority layers:
1. Trees & large shrubs (cross-linked to tree-id-north-america)
2. Edible, medicinal, and utility plants (with strong safety notes)
3. Common mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians
4. Invasive species that change management decisions
5. Seasonal calendar (what is identifiable / usable when)

Sources to prioritize for later collection:
- Missouri Department of Conservation species pages
- University of Missouri Extension
- Local bioregional checklists and herbarium data
- Existing paper field guides the user already owns (photograph / systematically note key pages for offline use)

Rule: every entry should answer “How do I know what this is when I am standing in front of it with no signal?”
