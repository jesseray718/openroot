# Path A — Flat Cardboard Snap-Fit Design Guide
**OpenRoot Aero-Disc v0.1**

All pieces are cut from flat cardboard. No tubes. Male/female interlocking tabs function like Lego connectors. Acetone + silicone solution is applied only to mating surfaces so that a continuous waterproof membrane forms across every joint.

## 1. Locked Dimensions

- Outer diameter: 780 mm
- Axial thickness: 190 mm
- Face area: 0.478 m²
- Number of starts: 7
- Channel hydraulic diameter: 10 mm
- Coil radius (R_HELIX): 25 mm
- Pitch: 80 mm
- Target open-area fraction: ≈ 0.45
- Greenhouse air gap: 25–35 mm

These values keep calculated ΔP ≤ 5 Pa at 0.18 m³ s⁻¹ while delivering NTU ≈ 4.5–5.

## 2. Greenhouse Air Gap (mandatory)

A clear membrane is spaced 25–35 mm in front of the black-painted absorber face. Purpose: greenhouse effect + additional buoyancy-driven airflow.

## 3. Piece List (flat patterns)

### Outer cylindrical wall
One rectangle: length ≈ 2450 mm, height 190 mm, with interlocking end tabs.

### Helical channel walls (7 starts)
Each start is a developed strip ≈ 420 mm long × 12 mm wide with periodic male/female tabs every 45–50 mm.

### Radial spacer rings
Thin annular pieces that lock the helical strips at correct radius and maintain pitch. 4–5 stations along the length.

### End-face rings
Two flat annular discs that close the ends and locate the starts.

### Greenhouse spacers
Simple 25–35 mm tall tabs around the perimeter of the front face.

## 4. Silicone / Acetone Timing (critical)

1. Cut all pieces completely dry.
2. Apply acetone + silicone solution only to the mating faces and tab surfaces.
3. Assemble while the solution is still slightly tacky. The liquid bridges every joint and becomes a continuous waterproof membrane once cured.
4. Do not heavily coat the interior channel surfaces.
5. Allow the membrane to cure fully before filling with open-cell black aerocement.

## 5. First Measurement Protocol

After cure measure volumetric flow and static pressure drop around 0.18 m³ s⁻¹ and record inlet/outlet temperatures. Open an Issue with the raw numbers.
