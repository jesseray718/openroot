## 2. What the System Can Do for the World (when scaled)

- Raise the material floor (nutrition, water, basic shelter, primary health) by redirecting small fractions of existing high-imbalance flows
- Deploy a planetary-scale open connectivity mesh at negligible percentage of current telecom revenue
- Restore agricultural autonomy through open seed + workshop networks
- Provide offline, high-η decision support and knowledge ranking with zero external dependency
- Coordinate voluntary cooperatives that cannot be captured by whales or owners
- Supply real-scenario problem resolution for disasters and cascading failures using local knowledge only
- Fuse primitive reliability with modern measurement so solutions degrade gracefully when advanced layers fail
- Keep all knowledge and tools forkable and free so any node can reproduce the yield

## 3. Free & Low-Barrier Resources That Can Fuel It

### Knowledge & Data (already free)
- USDA / USFS tree and plant fact sheets (public domain)
- Missouri Department of Conservation & University of Missouri Extension materials
- Permies wiki & high-value public threads
- Hackaday.io open projects with full BOMs
- Public-domain and openly licensed wisdom / proverb collections
- SIPRI, World Bank, WHO, and other public statistical series used in the redirection arithmetic

### Software & Protocols (already free)
- The entire openroot repository (CC-BY-SA docs, GPL code)
- Zero-dependency ledgers and agents already written
- llama.cpp / Ollama / open models for local inference
- Standard mesh and radio open protocols (LoRa, etc.)

### Funding & Support Channels to Investigate (public / competitive)
- Appropriate-technology and permaculture grants (various foundations and regional programs)
- Open-source hardware and civic-tech funds
- Community foundation and bioregional resilience grants
- Research / documentation grants that accept open notebooks and defensive publication
- Crowdfunding and mutual-aid pools that align with no-owner, public-good constraints
- In-kind: surplus materials, land access, workshop space, spectrum / tower access, recycled electronics

### Loan & Credit Instruments (use with caution)
- Only those that do not require surrender of the no-owner / open principles
- Prefer revenue-based or community underwriting models over extractive collateralization
- The business-leveling cooperative itself is designed as a non-extractive capital pool

### Human & Institutional Free Resources
- Existing maker spaces, libraries, extension offices, and amateur radio clubs
- Public domain and Creative Commons media for training
- Volunteer attestation networks (the two-node Agape rule)
- Students and researchers who need real open projects

## 5. Operating Rule for Resources
Every external grant, loan, or free resource is accepted only if it:
1. Does not create an owner or preferential claim above the ledger
2. Does not restrict the open licensing of knowledge and tools
3. Can be shown to raise the current lowest node
4. Leaves the system able to continue if that resource later disappears

## 6. Immediate Practical Use
- This document itself is a map for anyone evaluating whether to contribute time, material, spectrum, land, or money
- Mesh nodes can carry the capability list and the resource list so local groups know both what is possible and what support already exists
- Grant writers and mutual-aid organizers can start from the matching matrix instead of from zero
