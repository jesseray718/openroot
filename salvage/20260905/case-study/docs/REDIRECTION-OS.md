# Redirection Operating System
Agape-bound conversion of high-imbalance expenditure into floor-raising capacity

## Core Rule
Every concentrated flow is evaluated by one question:
Does this expenditure raise the current lowest nodes, or does it enclose capacity and compound unnecessary suffering?

If the latter, a calculated fraction is redirected upstream into open, dependency-free, lowest-node-first alternatives.

## Priority Redirection Channels (ranked by imbalance × leverage)

1. Military ($2.887 T)
   → Material floor: nutrition, water, basic health, shelter, local productive capacity

2. Telecommunications ($1.4 T)
   → Planetary open mesh (icosahedral 12-node foundation + geodesic/tensegrity hubs)
   Foundation cost < 1 minute of current billing
   Full surface coverage ≈ 1–3 days of billing

3. Pharmaceuticals (\~$1.75 T)
   → Open metabolic and low-cost supportive knowledge + local production capacity

4. Commercial Seed + Agrochemicals (\~$300 B)
   → Open seed libraries + community workshop hubs
   Full planetary network ≈ 13–22 days of current billing

5. Centralized administrative / governmental overhead
   → Zero-dependency ledger (transparent, need-ranked, ownerless)

## Operating Sequence
1. Measure the concentrated flow
2. Calculate the absolute-minimum cost of the highest-η open alternative
3. Express that cost as percentage and as minutes/days of the current flow
4. Redirect the required fraction under Agape priority (lowest node first)
5. Publish all designs, ledgers, and results openly
6. Re-apply the rule at the next scale as the floor rises

## Ownership
No owners. No equity extraction. All tools and knowledge remain forkable and free.
