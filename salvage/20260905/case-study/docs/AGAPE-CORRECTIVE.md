# Agape Corrective Mechanism

## Definition
1. Benefit is counted only when it lands on someone other than the actor.
2. First and permanent priority is the current lowest node.
3. Coordination power is legitimate only while bound by the above.
4. Knowledge and tools that raise the floor remain open and dependency-free.

## Operational Sequence
- Error signal = unnecessary suffering in the lowest nodes
- Surplus is preferentially directed to raise the current floor
- Public-benefit claims require independent attestation
- Successful methods are published openly
- As the floor rises, more nodes become capable of contributing; the rule is reapplied at the next scale

## What it prevents
Credit and industrial capacity used primarily for enclosure or domination.

## What it enables
Voluntary cooperative alliances that compound capacity while continuously reducing the gap between highest and lowest nodes.
