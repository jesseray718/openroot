# Case Study Full Chain
Euclid-style structure from universal axioms through historical pattern to present consequences

## Universal Axioms
A1 Existence and distinction
A2 Action and consequence
A3 Limited capacity
A4 Valuation
A5 Communication and coordination
A6 Error and correction

## Core Postulates
P1 Limited capacity forces priority decisions
P2 Ignoring the lowest nodes produces accumulating error
P3 Concentrated coordination amplifies both correction and error
P4 System health is measured by the direction of change in the worst-off
P5 Open knowledge increases corrective capacity; enclosed knowledge decreases it

## Propositions
Prop 1: Credit expansion beyond savings lowers the immediate political cost of large-scale war
Prop 2: The same concentrated credit and industrial capacity that finances war also encloses low-tech, decentralized alternatives

## Historical Applications
- Fractional-reserve and central-bank credit in the American Civil War, WWI, and WWII
- Rothschild network role in Napoleonic war finance (real, limited, not omnipotent)
- Gold standard purpose (constraint on money creation) and collapse (1971 Nixon Shock → fiat)
- Bitcoin white paper (peer-to-peer cash without institutions) versus later financialization and regulatory pressure
- Hemp suppression documented in The Emperor Wears No Clothes
- Parallel agricultural and medical consolidation (Flexner, seed/chemical systems)

## Present Scale
World military expenditure reached approximately $2.887 trillion in 2025 (SIPRI), 11th consecutive year of growth.

## Logical Conclusion
Concentrated coordination power unbound by the requirement that benefit be measured at the recipient recurrently expands both the capacity for industrial war and the obstruction of simple, high-η decentralized solutions. This compounds unnecessary suffering.

Corrective: open knowledge, dependency-free tools, and voluntary cooperative alliances that treat Agape (lowest node first, benefit measured only at the recipient) as non-negotiable source code.
