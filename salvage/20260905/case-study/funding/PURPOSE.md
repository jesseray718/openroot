# Funding Purpose

This collective exists to fund and maintain dependency-free tools and open knowledge that:

1. Document the pattern of centralized power creating complex problems with simple decentralized solutions.
2. Identify and propagate the highest-η countermeasures.
3. Support voluntary cooperative alliances that practice Agape as source code.
4. Remain owned by no one.

Capital is accepted only as contribution to the shared ledger. No equity, no preferential returns, no private extraction. All outputs stay open.
