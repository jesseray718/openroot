# Draft: Open Mesh Foundation (Digital Inclusion / Civic Tech)
**Ask focus:** Documentation, first regional deployment kit, and open training materials for the 12-node icosahedral + community hub mesh.

**Why this funder type:** Civic tech and digital inclusion programs seek connectivity that still works when commercial infrastructure fails.

**Core claim:** Full planetary foundation cost is <1 minute of current global telecom revenue; a regional pilot is a tiny fraction and immediately raises the lowest connectivity nodes.

**Deliverables to promise:**
- Complete open build + operations package
- First local / regional hubs online
- Offline knowledge payload (mesh-data seeds) pre-loaded
- Public report of cost, coverage, and lowest-node reach
