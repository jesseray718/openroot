- **Tier 2 (Meso)**: Cross-domain teams. Integrated flows (energy ledger + food production + compute scheduling + DeFi collateral).
- **Tier 3 (Macro)**: Governance + long-horizon. Quadratic ACRE voting, strategy, recursive self-improvement of the swarm itself.

Every tier uses identical UNE nomenclature, Kingdom Engine role taxonomy (Steward, Engineer, Oracle, Governor, Verifier), feedback loops, and verification primitives.
