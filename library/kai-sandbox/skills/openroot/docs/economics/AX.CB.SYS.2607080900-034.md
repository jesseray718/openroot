# AX-034 — The Tier Gate

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Credit score ranges function as gated tiers. Crossing a threshold (660→670→700→720→740) unlocks qualitatively different products — not just better rates on existing products, but entirely new categories of financial instruments. The marginal effort to cross a threshold yields discontinuous benefit, not linear improvement.

## Derivation

Lending products are gated by score brackets. A 669 vs 670 may differ by one point but unlock different product tiers entirely. This is a step function, not a gradient. The benefit of crossing a threshold is categorical (access to new instruments), not incremental (marginally better rates).

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-009 (Limit Expansion Cascade)
- PO-CB-005 (Convergence Point)

## Application

Identify the nearest threshold above current score. Focus all credit-building actions on crossing that specific gate. Do not dissipate effort across multiple goals when one threshold crossing unlocks the next tier of instruments.
