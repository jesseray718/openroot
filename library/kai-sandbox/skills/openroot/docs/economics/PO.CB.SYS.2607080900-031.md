# PO-031 — The Separation Singularity

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

The moment personal and business finances are fully separated — separate bank accounts, separate cards, separate payment routing — three cascading benefits activate simultaneously: (1) business credit begins building independently, (2) tax deductions become defensible, (3) personal liability is reduced through entity veil strength. The separation itself is a multiplicative event, not merely administrative housekeeping.

## Derivation

Pre-separation: business expenses mixed with personal → no business tradelines possible → deductions indefensible → no entity veil protection (piercing risk). Post-separation: business expenses isolated → business card tradelines report → D&B file builds → IRS audit defense solid → LLC veil reinforced by clean financial separation. Three independent benefits activate from a single action.

## Application

Open business bank account immediately. Route all business expenses through business card from that day forward. Never commingle funds after separation. The separation is a singularity — it changes the system state permanently.
