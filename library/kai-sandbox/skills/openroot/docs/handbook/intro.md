### What Is OpenRoot?

OpenRoot is an open-source framework applying permaculture principles to civilization's core infrastructure: energy, food, shelter, finance, governance, and information.

The core insight: **the same structural architecture governs all generative systems.** Credit-building follows the same laws as ecosystem design, which follows the same laws as community trust networks. When human systems align with these laws, they produce more life than they consume. When they violate them, they produce suffering.
