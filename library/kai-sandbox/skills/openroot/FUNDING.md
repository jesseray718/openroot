# Funding OpenRoot

> **ACRE tokens are never sold. They are earned only through verified physical work. Money funds bounties and materials — not tokens.**

OpenRoot is a zero-budget, solo-founded open-source project. Every dollar goes directly to physical prototyping, material costs, and bounty rewards for contributors who verify work.

## How to Fund

### Direct Support
- **GitHub Sponsors:** [github.com/sponsors/jesseray718](https://github.com/sponsors/jesseray718) *(enable in Settings → Sponsors)*
- **Contact:** jrm8908@proton.me for material donations, lab access, or equipment sponsorship

### Bounty-Funded Research
Fund a specific hypothesis verification:
- **WBTE-01 bench test** — evaporative cooling performance validation
- **CTBS-01 thermal cascade** — multi-night storage and Stirling discharge
- **AE-GFRC pour test** — aerated glass fiber reinforced concrete with zirconium substitution

Each bounty funds materials + shipping + a small stipend for the builder who completes and documents the test. Results are published openly under the same CC-BY-SA-4.0/GPL-3.0 licenses.
