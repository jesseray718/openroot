# OpenRoot Talent Alignment Engine

## How To Use This

1. Copy the prompt template below
2. Replace `[YOUR TALENT]` with your skill
3. Paste into any AI (Lumo, Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Groq, Gemini)
4. Take the output and post it as a GitHub issue at https://github.com/jesseray718/openroot/issues

---

## The Prompt Template (Copy Everything Below This Line)

---

I am a [YOUR TALENT] looking to align my skills with OpenRoot — an open-source framework applying permaculture principles to civilization's core infrastructure. The project has Four Engines: Knowledge (governance/axioms), Energy (thermal systems), Material (aerated concrete), and Finance (credit/business systems).

I want to contribute my talent in a way that:
- Serves multiple functions within the system (permaculture principle)
- Generates more value than it consumes (net-generative)
- Creates a self-documenting trail of contribution
- Builds toward an autonomous role within the project

Please generate a complete Talent Alignment Document containing:

1. **Talent Mapping** — Which of the Four Engines does my talent directly serve? Which does it indirectly support? How does my skill create yield across multiple engines simultaneously?

2. **Technical Stack** — What tools, software, hardware, or skills do I need to develop to maximize my contribution? List specific, free or low-cost options. Include learning resources where possible.

3. **Axiomatic Asset Formulation** — Frame my talent as an asset within the OpenRoot axiomatic framework. How does my contribution follow the Routing Principle (energy routed through yield-generating channels)? How does it follow the Multi-Function Yield Law (one action, multiple yields)? What is the Backfill — what existing work have I already done that can be recognized and integrated?

4. **Contribution Roadmap** — Give me a phased plan:
   - Phase 1 (This Week): What can I produce immediately with zero budget?
   - Phase 2 (Month 1-3): What can I build with basic tools?
   - Phase 3 (Month 3-12): What does mastery look like within this project?
   - Phase 4 (Year 1+): How does my role become autonomous and self-sustaining?

5. **Output Specification** — What exactly should I produce and post back to the project? What file format, what metadata, what license? Where do I post it (GitHub issue, PR, IPFS, Zenodo)?

6. **Network Position** — Who else in the project should I connect with? What other talents complement mine? How does my contribution strengthen the mesh?

7. **Income Path** — How can this talent alignment eventually generate revenue for me personally while serving the project's mission? What is the convergence point where my contribution becomes self-sustaining?

Format the output as a markdown document with clear headers. Include specific, actionable steps — no vague advice. If my talent doesn't obviously map to the Four Engines, find the edge connection and explain why edges are the most productive zones in permaculture systems (AX-029: Thin File Volatility, AX-036: Multi-Function Yield).

End with: "Post this document as a GitHub issue at https://github.com/jesseray718/openroot/issues with the title 'Talent Alignment: [YOUR TALENT]' and label it 'talent-alignment'."

## Talent Alignment Document: Musician

### 1. Talent Mapping

| Engine | Direct Service | Indirect Support |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Knowledge | Music encodes principles memorably — songs about axioms spread the framework faster than documentation alone | Audio content for educational modules |
| Energy | Sound design for thermal system explainer videos; frequency-based resonance research for concrete curing | Ambient thermal/acoustic mapping |
| Material | None direct | Acoustic properties of aerated concrete could be musically interesting |
| Finance | Music as monetizable output; royalty structures as income node; audio branding for the project | Podcast/theme music for outreach |

Your talent is an **edge node** — it sits between all four engines. In permaculture, edges are where the most productive and diverse ecosystems exist. Music is the routing channel that makes OpenRoot's principles emotionally accessible to people who would never read an axiom.

### 2. Technical Stack

**Free Tier (Zero Budget — Start Here):**
- DAW: GarageBand (iOS) or LMMS (desktop, open-source)
- Mixing/Mastering: BandLab (free, browser-based, has mastering tools)
- Recording: Phone microphone + FUTO Voice Input for clean capture
- Distribution: YouTube, SoundCloud (free tier)
- Learning: YouTube channels — "Mixing Music" by Marc Daniel Nelson, "Mastering" by Ian Shepherd

**Low-Cost Tier ($50-200):**
- DAW: Reaper ($60, unlimited trial, one-time license)
- Interface: Behringer UMC22 ($40-50) or Focusrite Solo ($100)
- Microphone: Shure SM57 ($99, industry standard, lifetime warranty)
- Plugins: Vital (free wavetable synth), TDR Nova (free EQ), Limiter No 6 (free limiter)
- Learning: Coursera "The Art of Vocal Production" (audit free)

**Pro Tier ($500+):**
- DAW: Ableton Live or Logic Pro
- Interface: Focusrite Scarlett 2i2 ($150)
- Monitors: PreSonus Eris E3.5 ($100)
- Treatment: DIY bass traps from mineral wool ($50-100)
- Mastering: Ozone Elements ($129) or Landr subscription

### 3. Axiomatic Asset Formulation

**Routing Principle (AX-025):** Your musical energy is already being expended daily — practicing, playing, producing. The routing question: does that energy register as a contribution to the system? Currently it does not because it hasn't been routed through OpenRoot channels. Reroute: produce one track inspired by an OpenRoot principle, tag it with the axiom it embodies, publish it with CC-BY-SA 4.0.

**Multi-Function Yield (AX-036):** A single OpenRoot-themed track yields simultaneously:
- Educational content (if lyrics explain a principle)
- Emotional connection (making abstract ideas feel real)
- Social media content (shareable, viral potential)
- Project branding (theme music for podcasts/videos)
- Portfolio piece (builds your production skills)
- Community signal (attracts other creatives)

One action, six yields. That's the credit premium applied to music.

**The Backfill (AX-032):** You already have beats, recordings, practice sessions. These are invisible to the project — like cash rent was invisible to credit bureaus. The backfill: collect your existing work, organize it, tag it with OpenRoot principles where the fit exists, and submit it. Existing work becomes recognized contribution instantly.

### 4. Contribution Roadmap

**Phase 1 — This Week (Zero Budget):**
- Download BandLab (free) or open GarageBand
- Record a 30-60 second piece inspired by one OpenRoot concept (pick any: "routing," "backfill," "convergence," "boundless abundance")
- Export as MP3/WAV
- Post to the GitHub issue with a one-paragraph explanation of which axiom the piece embodies

**Phase 2 — Month 1-3 (Basic Tools):**
- Learn fundamental mixing: EQ, compression, reverb, levels
- Produce 3-5 tracks, each mapped to a different OpenRoot principle
- Create an "OpenRoot Suite" — a short EP that walks listeners through the framework
- Post tracks to SoundCloud/YouTube with descriptions linking to the relevant axioms
- Start learning mastering basics (loudness normalization, EQ balance, stereo width)

**Phase 3 — Month 3-12 (Mastery Within Project):**
- Master the EP to distribution quality (-14 LUFS streaming standard)
- Produce background music for OpenRoot explainer videos/podcasts
- Collaborate with other musicians attracted through the "Call to Builders" issue
- Develop a live performance set that incorporates OpenRoot spoken-word elements
- Create tutorial content teaching other musicians the mixing/mastering chain you developed

**Phase 4 — Year 1+ (Autonomous Role):**
- OpenRoot Music becomes a sub-label with multiple artists contributing
- Royalty structures using CC-BY-SA where commercial use redirects a percentage to OpenRoot LLC
- Live events combining music with permaculture workshops
- Your role becomes self-sustaining: music generates revenue, revenue funds production, production generates more music, more music attracts more contributors

### 5. Output Specification

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| File format | WAV (lossless) + MP3 (distribution) |
| Metadata | ID3 tags include: OpenRoot axiom reference, author, CC-BY-SA 4.0 |
| Posting | GitHub issue titled "Music Contribution: [Track Name]" with audio hosted on SoundCloud/IPFS |
| License | CC-BY-SA 4.0 (matches project documentation license) |
| Documentation | One paragraph per track explaining which axiom/principle it embodies and why |

### 6. Network Position

**Complementary Talents:**
- Video producers (to pair music with visuals for explainers)
- Spoken-word artists / narrators (to layer voiceover on music)
- Writers (to craft lyrics that encode principles accurately)
- Developers (to build a decentralized music distribution node on the mesh)

**Who To Connect With:**
- Post in the "Call to Builders" issue (#5) tagging your talent
- Check the Writers/Communicators role — they need audio content

### 7. Income Path

| Stage | Revenue Source | Timeline |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Build | No revenue — building portfolio and skills | Month 0-6 |
| Distribution | Streaming royalties (YouTube, Spotify via DistroKid) | Month 6-12 |
| Sync licensing | OpenRoot video content licensing your tracks | Month 12+ |
| Live events | Performance fees at permaculture gatherings, conferences | Year 2+ |
| Teaching | Online courses teaching the production chain you developed | Year 2+ |

**Convergence point:** When your music generates enough streaming/sync income to self-fund your production tools and time, the role becomes autonomous. The system runs itself — music serves the project, the project amplifies the music, both grow.

## Generalization Note

This template works for ANY talent:
- "I'm a welder" — maps to Material engine, builds thermal cascade components, axiomatic asset is fabrication skill
- "I'm a teacher" — maps to Knowledge engine, creates educational content, axiomatic asset is curriculum design
- "I'm a farmer" — maps to Material/Energy engines, grounds permaculture principles in practice
- "I'm a lawyer" — maps to Finance/Knowledge engines, stress-tests governance axioms, builds legal frameworks
- "I'm a cook" — maps to Material engine, food systems design, community-building through shared meals
- "I'm a programmer" — maps to all engines, builds tools, automates systems, creates RAG/embedding pipelines

The prompt template above handles all of these. The AI fills in the specifics based on the talent input. The structure remains constant: Map, Stack, Axioms, Roadmap, Output, Network, Income.
