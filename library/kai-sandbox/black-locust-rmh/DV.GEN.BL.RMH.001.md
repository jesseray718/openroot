# DV.GEN.BL.RMH.001
## Black Locust Coppicing + Rocket Mass Heater
### Function
- Annual coppice harvest from Robinia pseudoacacia feeds high-efficiency Rocket Mass Heater
- Output: Dispatchable radiant heat (12-24h storage via thermal mass) + Stirling electricity from waste heat
- Net carbon negative: root system continues sequestering while shoots are harvested and burned
- Efficiency: 90-95% combustion, negligible particulates

### Integration with H-003 Thermal Cascade
Replaces or augments the solar-thermal input path:
BLACK LOCUST COPPICE (annual harvest) → ROCKET MASS HEATER → [PRIMARY] radiant heat for habitation
                                           ↓
                                    [SECONDARY] underground labyrinth thermal storage
                                           ↓
                                    [TERTIARY] Stirling engine → electricity
                                           ↓
                                    [EXHAUST] greenhouse/water warming + CO₂ for plants

Works 24/7 (not solar-dependent). Uses marginal/degraded land. Creates additional yields (honey, food forest understory, pollinator habitat).

### Key Advantages over Solar-Only
- Dispatchable (burn when needed)
- Carbon negative (root sequestration continues)
- Wealth retention (local labor + fuel vs imported energy spend)
- Compatible with AE-GFRC domes and aquaponics (exhaust heat recovery)
