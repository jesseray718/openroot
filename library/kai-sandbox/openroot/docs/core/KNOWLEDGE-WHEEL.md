# Knowledge Wheel v1 — forkable index (the wheel is the index, not the territory)
Rule: fractal — any spoke forks to its own repo when depth demands. Format:
domain | governing SI quantities | feeds module | best free source
- Thermodynamics | K, J, W, W/(m²·K) | solar panel, labyrinth, Stirling | OpenStax Physics, NIST WebBook
- Materials science | Pa, MPa, kg/m³ | volumetric concrete, ferrocement | MIT OCW 3.091, MatWeb
- Structural mechanics | N, Pa, N·m | domes, catenary, cardboard panels | OpenStax, Engineering ToolBox
- Fluid dynamics | m³/s, Pa | labyrinth airflow, cistern plumbing | MIT OCW 2.06
- Hydrology/water | m³, L/s, mm rainfall | catchment → 50k-gal tanks | Appropedia, TX Rainwater Manual
- Soil/agronomy | pH, kg/ha | bio-intensive growing | FAO docs, Appropedia
- Energy conversion | W, J, η% | Stirling, thermal loop | DOE OSTI, NASA NTRS
- Computation/networks | B, Hz, W | Kai, AEGIS MESH, node zero | Kiwix, MDN
- Economics/commons | ACRE, labor-hr | ACRE ledger, The Hearth | Ostrom (Governing the Commons)
- Human factors/safety | °C comfort, dB, lux | all dwellings | OSHA, WHO housing guidelines
