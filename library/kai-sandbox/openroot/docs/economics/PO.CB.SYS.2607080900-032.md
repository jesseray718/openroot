# PO-032 — The Compound Timeline

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Postulate
**Status:** Active

## Statement

From zero business credit to commercial property qualification under DSCR, the minimum viable timeline is approximately 18-24 months assuming: DUNS filed Month 1, secured business card Month 1-2, Net-30 vendors Month 2-4, PAYDEX 80 by Month 6, unsecured business card Month 6-12, business auto loan Month 12-18, DSCR loan Month 18-24. Each phase is dependent on the prior. The timeline cannot be compressed below these gates but can be extended by inaction at any node.

## Derivation

DUNS generation: 30 days. Secured card approval + first report: 60 days. Net-30 establishment + PAYDEX: 90-120 days. Unsecured card qualification: 6+ months banking history. Business auto loan: 12+ months business credit. DSCR loan: 18+ months business operation + established PAYDEX + personal score 720+. Each gate has a minimum time floor driven by bureau reporting cycles and lender underwriting requirements.

## Application

Map all gates to a calendar. Execute each phase on schedule. The timeline is fixed — inaction at any node extends the total timeline by the delay duration. The fastest path is immediate execution at every gate.
