# AX-038 — The Utilization Reset Postulate

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Because utilization has no memory, a credit profile can be maintained in a suboptimal state (high utilization, tight cash flow) indefinitely without permanent damage, then optimized to target utilization in the cycle preceding any score-dependent action (loan application, rental application). The strategy is: build history continuously, optimize utilization tactically.

## Derivation

Combination of AX-CB-002 (utilization has no memory) and AX-CB-003 (payment history is primary). Since payment history accrues permanently and utilization resets each cycle, the optimal strategy splits into two modes: (1) Build mode — accept suboptimal utilization, collect payment history; (2) Optimize mode — reduce utilization before score-dependent action.

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-002 (Utilization Threshold)
- AX-CB-003 (Payment History Primacy)
- PO-CB-011 (Imperfect Utilization Superiority)

## Application

During cash-constrained periods, do not avoid using credit instruments out of utilization concerns. Continue building payment history. Before any loan application, reduce balances to under 10% for 1-2 cycles. The score will reflect the optimized state.
