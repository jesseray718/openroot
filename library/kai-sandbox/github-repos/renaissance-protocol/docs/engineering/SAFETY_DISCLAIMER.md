# ⚠️ Safety Disclaimer — Renaissance Protocol

**READ BEFORE BUILDING**

## Warning
The Aerocement Passive Thermal Kingdom (APTK) is **experimental technology**. This information is provided for educational and research purposes only.

## Critical Hazards
1. **HHO Gas:** Explosive when mixed with air. Never store. Use flashback arrestors. On-demand generation only.
2. **Cement:** Caustic. Wear gloves, mask, and eye protection.
3. **Excavation:** Labyrinth construction involves digging 10ft deep tunnels. Risk of collapse. Follow local building codes.
4. **Electrical:** Stirling engine generators produce electricity. Proper grounding and insulation required.

## Liability
By building this system, you accept full responsibility for:
- Personal injury or death
- Property damage
- Legal compliance with local regulations
- All risks associated with experimental technology

## Recommendation
- Consult with licensed engineers before large-scale builds.
- Obtain necessary permits.
- Start with small prototypes.
- Document everything.
