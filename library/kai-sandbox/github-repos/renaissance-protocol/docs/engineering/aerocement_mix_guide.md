# Open-Cell Aerocement Mix Guide
**Version:** 1.0
**Goal:** Create a breathable, high-surface-area cement matrix for passive thermal systems.

## Ingredients
1. **Portland Cement:** 2 parts (by volume)
2. **Xanthan Gum Slurry:** 15g gum dissolved in rubbing alcohol
3. **Dawn Ultra Gel:** 1/4 cup Dawn + water (to make a no-slump gel)
4. **Activated Carbon:** Optional (for blackbody 98% absorption)

## Procedure
1. **Prepare Slurry:** Mix 15g Xanthan gum in rubbing alcohol.
2. **Prepare Gel:** Mix 1/4 cup Dawn Ultra with water until it forms a gel with almost no slump.
3. **Combine:** Mix the slurry and gel together.
4. **Add Carbon (Optional):** If making blackbody structure, add activated carbon to the gel *before* adding cement.
5. **Mix with Cement:** Combine gel mixture with cement at a **1:2 ratio** (Gel:Cement).
6. **Aeration:** Stir vigorously. 
   - *Observation:* You will see microscopic bubbles form.
   - *Critical Point:* When bubbles begin to pop rapidly upon stopping, you have reached the **Open-Cell Transition Volume**. Stop mixing here.
7. **Cure:** Pour into molds. Allow to cure naturally.

## Properties
- **Porosity:** High (capillary action)
- **Strength:** Derived from microscopic spherical air voids
- **Thermal:** High emissivity/absorptivity (with carbon)
- **Breathability:** Allows gaseous and liquid exchange

## Safety
- Wear gloves and mask when handling cement.
- Work in well-ventilated area.
- Test small batches before full-scale builds.
