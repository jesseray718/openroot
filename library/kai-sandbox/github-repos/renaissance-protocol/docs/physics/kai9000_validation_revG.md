## 2. Fuel Balance - Full Derivation

### 2.1 Night Cooling Requirements

To sustain 75 Pa vacuum for 12 hours of darkness, a 40m chimney is required.
- Required Thermal Input: ~730 kW (from 10 Rocket Mass Heaters in parallel).
- Fuel Needed: ~2.3 tonnes of dry biomass per night.
- Annual Fuel Need: ~730 tonnes.

### 2.2 The "1 Acre" Correction

- Original Claim: 1 acre Black Locust + 50k gal algae = sufficient fuel.
- Reality: 1 acre provides only 1.25% of the required energy.
- Corrected Requirement: 91 acres of Black Locust coppice (3-year rotation) OR 25 acres of high-yield algae ponds.
- Feasibility: For a 100-home community, 91 acres is ~0.9 acres/home. This is standard rural land availability.

### 2.3 Optimal Fuel Mix

- 70% Black Locust Coppice: High BTU, fast growth, nitrogen-fixing, bee forage.
- 20% Algae Cake: High energy density, grows in wastewater, captures CO2.
- 10% Wood Waste: Prunings, deadfall.
