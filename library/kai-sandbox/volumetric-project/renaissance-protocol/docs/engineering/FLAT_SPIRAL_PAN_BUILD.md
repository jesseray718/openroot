# FLAT SPIRAL PAN: CUTTING & ASSEMBLY GUIDE
**Material:** Corrugated Plastic Box (Coroplast)
**Goal:** A shallow pan with a spiral channel inside for air to flow through thin cement walls.
**Sun shines THROUGH the clear top, INTO the channel.**

---

## 1. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Imagine a baking pan with a spiral maze inside.
- Air enters at the outer edge.
- Follows the spiral path inward.
- Exits at the center.
- The cement walls are thin (~1.5 inches).
- The clear top lets sun heat the ENTIRE channel.

---

## 2. CUT LIST

From your Coroplast box, cut these pieces:

**Piece A - Base:**
- Size: 18 x 18 inches (square)
- This is the bottom of the pan

**Piece B - Outer Walls (4 strips):**
- Size: 18 inches long x 2 inches tall each
- These form the 4 sides of the pan

**Piece C - Spiral Divider:**
- Cut ONE long strip: 2 inches wide x ~120 inches long
- (That is 10 feet. Cut multiple strips and tape them together.)
- This strip will bend into the spiral shape inside the pan

**Piece D - Clear Top:**
- Size: 18 x 18 inches (clear plastic wrap, ziplock bag, or acrylic sheet)
- Must be transparent. Sun must shine through.

---

## 3. ASSEMBLY

### Step 1: Build the Pan
1. Take Piece A (Base).
2. Glue Piece B strips around all 4 edges with silicone.
3. You now have a shallow box: 18x18x2 inches.

### Step 2: Layout the Spiral
1. Starting at one corner of the pan, place the end of Piece C (the long strip).
2. Curve it inward in a spiral pattern.
3. Each turn of the spiral should be about 3 inches apart (measured wall-to-wall).
4. This creates a channel 3 inches wide for air to flow.
5. Glue the spiral strip down to the base with silicone at every contact point.

### Step 3: Cut Openings
1. **Inlet:** Cut a 2-inch hole in the outer wall where the spiral starts.
2. **Outlet:** Cut a 2-inch hole in the center (or nearby wall) where the spiral ends.

### Step 4: Pour Cement
1. Mix aerocement (follow Mixing Guide).
2. Pour into the spiral CHANNELS only (between the walls).
3. Fill to 1.5 inches deep (leave 0.5 inch of air space above the cement).
4. DO NOT fill the air channel. The channel must stay OPEN for airflow.

### Step 5: Seal the Top
1. Place the clear plastic sheet (Piece D) over the entire pan.
2. Tape or silicone seal ALL edges.
3. No air leaks. The only openings are the inlet and outlet holes.

---

## 4. HOW IT WORKS

1. Sun shines through the clear top.
2. Hits the black carbon-infused cement (absorbs 95% of heat).
3. Cement heats the air inside the channel.
4. Hot air rises toward the outlet (thermosiphon effect).
5. Cool air is drawn in through the inlet.
6. The spiral path makes the air travel a LONG distance (slow, high heat transfer).
7. Temperature at outlet should be 3-7°C above ambient.

---

## 5. TESTING

1. Place the pan in DIRECT sunlight.
2. Put a thermometer at the inlet (ambient air).
3. Put a thermometer at the outlet (heated air).
4. Wait 15 minutes for steady state.
5. Record: Outlet Temp MINUS Inlet Temp = DELTA-T.
6. **TARGET: Delta-T >= 3.7°C**

---

## 6. TIPS
- If Delta-T is too LOW: Make the spiral tighter (more turns = longer path).
- If air is NOT flowing: Tilt the pan slightly (outlet higher than inlet) to help thermosiphon.
- If cement is TOO THICK: Pour thinner next time. Thin cement heats faster.
- Spray the Coroplast with cooking oil before pouring. Cement won't stick as much.

**End of Build Guide.**
