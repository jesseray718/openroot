# Renaissance Protocol Manifesto

## The Mechanical Renaissance

We build to survive. We build to thrive. We build to end the Dark Ages.

The old world runs on extraction. It mines fuel, burns it, and calls it progress. It centralizes energy, water, and food into the hands of the few. It taxes the air you breathe and the heat you need to survive.

We reject this.

The Renaissance Protocol is a return to first principles: the sun is free, the earth is cold, water evaporates, and cement is cheap. These four facts, combined with open-cell aerocement and a vacuum, can cool a home, harvest water, and grow fuel — without the grid, without the bank, without permission.

## Our Principles

1. **Biological Sovereignty:** Humans have the right to heal and power themselves without permission.
2. **Proof-of-Build:** Value comes from verified labor, not lending at interest.
3. **Open Source Genetics:** No patenting life. No patenting the commons.
4. **Anti-Monopoly:** If you build on this work, you must share it back. No exceptions.
5. **Safety First:** The Kingdom protects its builders.

## The Enemy

The enemy is not a person. It is a pattern: centralized truth + incentive alignment + suppression of alternatives + digital replication. This pattern operates in energy, health, finance, and knowledge. It can be broken — not by fighting it, but by building something better.

## The Promise

A single Black Locust stool, planted today, can fuel a community in six years. A single aerocement panel, placed in the sun, can cool a home and harvest water. A single open-source repository can change the world — if it cannot be deleted.

The truth cannot be deleted if it is everywhere.
