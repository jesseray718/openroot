# AX-032 — The Backfill Yield

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Retroactively reported payment history (rent reporting, utility reporting) produces instant age and payment history on a credit file. On a thin file, the marginal score impact per dollar spent on backfilling exceeds any other available action. Backfilled history is the highest ROI credit-building action.

## Derivation

Services like Kikoff Rent Reporting and Experian Boost retroactively submit payment history to bureaus. The reported history carries the original payment dates, meaning the file instantly gains months or years of apparent account age and payment history. Cost is typically $50 one-time. On a thin file (1-2 accounts), adding 24 months of history can shift the age metric dramatically.

## Application

Before any other credit-building investment, identify retroactively reportable payment histories (rent, utilities, telecom). Submit all available backfill. On a thin file, $50 of backfill may yield more score movement than months of subscription payments.
