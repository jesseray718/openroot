# AX-028 — The Dual Profile Separation

**Domain:** Credit & Business (CB)
**Type:** Axiom
**Status:** Active

## Statement

Personal credit (SSN-based) and business credit (EIN-based) are independent scoring systems maintained by separate bureaus. They do not cross-contaminate unless a personal guarantee is invoked. Maximum cascade requires parallel construction of both profiles simultaneously.

## Derivation

Personal credit bureaus: Equifax, Experian, TransUnion (SSN-keyed). Business credit bureaus: Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Business (EIN-keyed). These are separate databases with separate scoring algorithms. A personal guarantee links them temporarily for a specific obligation but does not merge the profiles.

## Cross-References

- AX-CB-011 (Business Credit Sequence)
- PO-CB-012 (Separation Singularity)

## Application

Build both profiles in parallel from day one. Never mix personal and business finances once separation is achieved. Use personal credit instruments for personal expenses, business credit instruments for business expenses.
