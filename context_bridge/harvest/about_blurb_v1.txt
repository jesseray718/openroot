OpenRoot is a local-first compute and energy system that measures everything
in joules. It runs specialized language models on recycled hardware, chains
every claim through SHA-256 ledgers that verify their own integrity, and
mints a work-backed currency (acre) only when energy is demonstrably
captured, stored, and used locally. The goal: demonstrate that
civilization-grade infrastructure — thermal storage, power generation,
computation, knowledge formalization — can be built, audited, and published
by one person, one desktop, and one phone.
