A mesh of useful work

Let the next community start further ahead.

OpenRoot aims to connect local nodes through a distributed knowledge commons. Ordinary phones and used computers should be able to preserve source material, search evidence, carry reusable workflows, and exchange updates when connectivity allows.

Offline operation and correction-aware reuse are design priorities. A complete independently adopted mesh of clusters is an intended outcome, not an established deployment claim.

Zero-cost-recompute ambition

Do not repeatedly pay the full cost of rediscovery. Retrieve a suitable result, verify its conditions and dependencies, and recompute only when necessary.

This means minimizing repeated work and marginal access cost—not literally free energy, storage, or computation.

Evidence, attribution, and exchange

Connect the record to the real outcome.

Telemetry and joule accounting

Record methods, calibration, operating conditions, uncertainty, inputs, losses, and useful output. Keep measured energy distinct from estimates and simulations.

A sensor record is evidence to assess, not automatic proof.

Cryptographic contribution ledger

Link artifacts, licenses, authors, dependencies, tests, reviews, replications, and corrections. Signatures and hashes support attribution and tamper detection under their verification assumptions.

Blockchain anchoring or shared settlement may support this architecture; neither makes a physical claim true.

ACRE

An experimental contribution and exchange concept within the broader project. Recognition of work, physical measurement, and transferable claims must remain distinct.

This page verifies no token contract, issuance, backing, redemption, or financial value. No return is promised.

Useful participation and evidence access should not require purchasing a token. Publish the rules, preserve attribution, make disputes and corrections visible, and prevent duplicate claims before connecting rewards to recorded outcomes.

The next connected proof

One bounded experiment. One source-linked evidence packet. Independent reuse on a second node. A correction that reaches that node without erasing attribution.

Reliable end-to-end correction-aware retrieval, disconnected pathway reuse, physical-system performance, independent cluster adoption, and ACRE economics require their own demonstrations.

Published designs are not structural, combustion, electrical, potable-water, or pressure-system safety certification.

A public workshop

Bring one useful improvement.

Reproduce a test. Document a failed mix. Calibrate a measurement. Review an energy balance. Repair an instruction. Help someone else use the result.
