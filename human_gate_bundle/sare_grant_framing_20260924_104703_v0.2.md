# SARE Farmer/Rancher Grant Proposal Framing — Integrated Thermal Cascade Farm
Status: DRAFT v0.2 (staged, human-gated) | License: CC-BY-SA-4.0 | Tier: $15k

## The problem (producer-facing)
Small diversified farms in the Missouri Bootheel and southeast Missouri pay
their thinnest margins twice: winter heat (tilapia water, quail airspace,
microgreens benches) purchased as propane, and summer losses (heat-stressed
livestock and stagnant cash flow between harvests). The energy floor is the
margin floor — and the smallholder pays the highest effective rate.

## The producer
Jesse McMillen Ray, OpenRoot LLC — open-source appropriate-technology commons.
Producer-led, producer-operated pilot on own site in southeast Missouri
(zone 7a; ~4.5-5 peak sun-hours annual average). All designs, simulations,
and results published GPL/CC with version-controlled evidence (PoPW).

## The system (integrated thermal + nutrient cascade)
Energy backbone: PASSIVE solar thermal cascade (glazed aerocement absorber
+ underground labyrinth storage), audited open simulation, thermosiphon —
no pumps, no fuel, no pressure vessels (process steam EXCLUDED by design).
Backup/black-locust RMH: carbon-negative supplemental heat from coppiced
black locust — a nitrogen-fixing hardwood that is simultaneously premier
bee forage. Delivered heat targets (per audited sim v6.3, calibrated to
SE Missouri insolation): winter water/airspace warming for —

- Tilapia in ferrocement tanks with duckweed biofiltration (duckweed = feed)
- Quail towers (manure feeds vermiculture)
- Red wiggler composting (castings feed microgreens/aeroponic towers)
- Microgreens + aeroponic towers (high-margin winter cash crops)
- Freeze-drying + essential oil distillation (solar-thermal-assisted processing)
- Value-added: beeswax candles with farm-grown aromatics (outreach demo unit)

Nutrient cascade closes the loop: near-zero-waste operation.

## What the $15k funds
1. Materials for one instrumented pilot season: glazing, aerocement
   chemistry, thermocouple arrays, datalogger, flow measurement
2. Season-long measurement: delivered kWh-thermal vs. propane baseline
3. Independent verification of COP-boundary absorber performance
   (95%+ framed as collector-absorptance within control volume, never >100%)

## Deliverables (all public, replicable)
1. BOM at three cost tiers (scrap / retail / new)
2. Aerocement mix spec + construction drawings (plain-language)
3. Raw instrumented dataset, sha-chained, in-repo
4. Replication guide + field-day demonstration; farmers replicate the
   ENERGY CASCADE or any component independently (modular by design)

## Credibility (negative results published)
The audited simulation REFUSES unphysical performance: v6.3 sweep found
zero passive configs reaching 264C process steam, so the design pivoted
to honest low-grade heat delivery — heating water and air, not boiling.
Our published refusals are the reason to trust our claims.

## Risks stated plainly
- AR-GFRC freeze-thaw durability (zone 7a test plan included in season)
- Aquaponics thermal load varies with stocking density (measured, modeled)
- Single-site pilot; replication guides mitigate regional variance
