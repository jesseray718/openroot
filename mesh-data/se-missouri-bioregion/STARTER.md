# SE Missouri Bioregion — Starter Notes

Priority tree / plant groups to complete first:
- Bottomland hardwoods (cottonwood, sycamore, boxelder, silver maple, green ash where still present)
- Upland oaks and hickories
- Common edge species (persimmon, pawpaw, mulberry, black locust, cedar)
- Aggressive invasives that change management (privet, callery pear, certain honeysuckles, etc.)

Wildlife of first practical interest:
- White-tailed deer, raccoon, opossum, common snakes, major bird groups, feral hogs where present

Water:
- Ditch and bootheel hydrology awareness
- Surface vs. shallow groundwater considerations
- Seasonal flood / drought pattern notes
