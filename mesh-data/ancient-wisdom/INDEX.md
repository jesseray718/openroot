# Proverbs & Ancient Wisdom Seed
Focus: compressed, time-tested decision heuristics that remain useful under stress and low information.

Sources to prioritize:
1. Agricultural and seasonal proverbs (many cultures)
2. Stoic and practical ethics short texts
3. Biblical / wisdom literature decision rules (especially mutual aid and lowest-node priority)
4. Indigenous land-care and reciprocity maxims (public-domain or properly attributed)
5. Folk engineering and weather lore that has measurable accuracy

Packaging:
- One short text or proverb cluster per file
- Tag by domain: food, water, conflict, scarcity, cooperation, timing
- Cross-link to modern survival and permaculture actions where the ancient rule still holds

Goal: a lightweight offline “oracle” of compressed heuristics for mesh nodes.
