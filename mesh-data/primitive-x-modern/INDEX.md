# Primitive × Modern Cross-Reference Seed
Purpose: fuse time-tested low-tech principles with current materials, measurement, and information tools.

Examples of high-value fusions:
- Primitive shelter geometry + modern moisture/vapor control knowledge
- Traditional cordage + modern high-strength fibers / recycling of existing cordage
- Observational weather lore + simple digital logging (when power exists)
- Gravity and thermal mass cooling + current passive-house / earth-tube data
- Natural indicator species + basic water-quality test strips or sensor data
- Scavenged metal / glass + modern fastening and sealing techniques
- Traditional food preservation + current botulism and pH safety rules

Structure each entry as:
- Primitive principle
- Modern measurement or material that improves reliability
- How the combination raises the lowest node (safety, yield, or reduced labor)
- What still works when the modern layer is removed

This seed is the bridge between the ancient-wisdom, permies, hackaday, and survival-scenario sets.
