# Primitive × Modern Worked Examples

1. Thermal mass cooling
   Primitive: earth contact, night flush, thick walls
   Modern: simple temperature logging, vapor control knowledge, reflective barriers
   Yield: lower energy, still works when power dies

2. Cordage + fastening
   Primitive: natural fiber technique and geometry
   Modern: scavenged high-strength line, correct knots + modern tape/sealants where appropriate
   Yield: faster secure lashings, graceful fallback to pure natural fiber

3. Water reliability
   Primitive: source selection, settling, boiling
   Modern: basic test strips, known contact times, container hygiene rules
   Yield: fewer failures under uncertainty

4. Knowledge under stress
   Primitive: compressed proverbs / heuristics
   Modern: offline ranked cards + scenario resolver
   Yield: faster correct prioritization when cognition is degraded
