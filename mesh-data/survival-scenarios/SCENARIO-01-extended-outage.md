# Scenario 01 — Extended Power Outage (days to weeks)

Trigger: Grid down > 12 h, no restoration ETA, or cascading outages.
Immediate (first 60 min):
- Life safety: check vulnerable people first (elderly, medical devices, children)
- Preserve cold chain only for critical medicines; eat highest-risk refrigerated food first
- Establish light + communication hierarchy (hand crank / battery / car only for critical)
- Water: confirm pressure; fill clean containers while residual pressure exists

Next 24 h:
- Water plan (stored + alternative sources + treatment method)
- Heat/cool plan appropriate to season (layering, shading, one warm room)
- Neighborhood lowest-node check
- Information discipline: one reliable source, limited radio time

Next 7–30 days:
- Food shift to non-perishable + local
- Sanitation plan if water/sewer degraded
- Skill and tool sharing via local ledger or paper
- Re-evaluate medical needs

Failure modes: generator fuel exhaustion, information panic, ignoring lowest nodes, unsafe alternative heat.
Cross-ref: primitive×modern (thermal mass, passive cooling), permies (wood heat principles), ancient-wisdom (conserve, prioritize weak).
